What affects the cost of a local move in Prince William County?

1. Size of the home and amount of belongings. More rooms and more furniture mean more time.

2. Stairs and long carries. Multi-level townhomes are common across Woodbridge and Dale City, and every flight of stairs adds time. Long walks from the truck to your door do too.

3. Elevators and building rules. Apartment and condo buildings may require elevator reservations or restrict move hours.

4. Distance and traffic. Even short moves can take longer when I-95, Route 1, or the Prince William Parkway are backed up.

5. Packing and unpacking. Packing services save you time but add to the total.

6. Specialty and heavy items. Pianos, safes, gym equipment, and other heavy pieces can require extra care and time. Mention them when you request your quote.

7. Timing. Demand and availability vary. Ask about alternate dates; a weekday or mid-month move does not guarantee a lower price.

8. Route and service scope. Confirm the applicable pricing and operating authority for your route; do not assume local package terms apply to every move.

Local movers vs. labor-only helpers

Some people hire labor-only help to load or unload a truck or container. Labor-only service may exclude transportation, rental-truck costs, fuel, and equipment. Compare the total cost and included services with a crew-and-truck package.

How to compare moving quotes

Ask for a written estimate that lists the first-two-hours price, the hourly rate after that, crew size, truck size, and any add-on charges. At Haul Bros, a studio or 1-bedroom gets 2 movers, a 2 or 3-bedroom gets 3, and a 4-bedroom gets 4, all with a 26-ft truck.

Ask what's included: blankets, shrink wrap, floor protection, dollies, standard furniture assembly.

Ask how the estimate is calculated and what could change the final total.

Compare total cost, not just the lowest advertised price.

Be cautious of a very low quote. Some movers advertise a bargain and then add fees after your belongings are loaded.

Verify the operating authority applicable to your route and request insurance evidence. Haul Bros lists USDOT #4497497; a USDOT number alone does not establish authorization for every service.

8 ways to lower your moving cost

Be ready when the crew arrives. Boxes sealed, furniture cleared, and parking arranged means you're paying for moving, not waiting.

Declutter first. Fewer items means fewer hours.

Pack yourself, if you have time, and save packing help for fragile items.

Disassemble what you can before moving day, or ask for standard assembly help.

Reserve elevators and parking so the crew isn't held up.

Move on a weekday or mid-month when the schedule is more flexible.

Ask about current discounts and confirm eligibility in your estimate.

Get everything in writing so there are no surprises.
