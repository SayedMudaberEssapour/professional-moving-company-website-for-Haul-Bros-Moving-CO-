Moving in Arlington requires detailed preparation. Many apartment and condominium buildings have freight-elevator reservations, certificates of insurance, loading-dock schedules, parking restrictions, designated moving hours, and strict move-in procedures.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about moving in Arlington with Haul Bros.

How much do movers cost in Arlington, VA?

Moving costs depend on crew size, total time, inventory, stairs, elevators, walking distance, parking, and packing services. Haul Bros starts at $395 for the first two hours with two movers and a truck, followed by $149 for each additional hour. Confirm all pricing through a current written estimate.

Do Arlington apartment buildings require elevator reservations?

Yes, many high-rise and condominium buildings require residents to reserve a freight elevator and loading area in advance. Some buildings also require a certificate of insurance (COI), refundable move deposit, and protective floor coverings.

Can Haul Bros load or unload my rental truck?

Yes. Labor-only crews can load or unload customer-rented trucks, trailers, and storage containers. Provide the truck size, inventory, stairs, and parking details before receiving your estimate.

How far in advance should I book Arlington movers?

Four to six weeks is recommended for an ordinary local move. Summer dates, weekends, holidays, and month-end dates fill quickly, especially for buildings with restricted elevator schedules.
