Junk Removal FAQs

What types of junk do you remove?

Eligible furniture, appliances, mattresses, boxes, household goods, office furniture, storage contents, and moving debris.

Do you remove furniture and appliances?

Yes, depending on item size, condition, location, and disposal requirements.

Do you remove items from apartments?

Yes. Confirm building access, elevator, loading, parking, and property-management requirements.

Do you clean storage units?

Yes. We can remove eligible furniture, boxes, appliances, and household belongings from storage units.

Can you remove one item?

We may remove one furniture item, appliance, mattress, or other large item depending on details.

Do you offer junk removal during a move?

Yes. Junk removal can be scheduled separately or discussed as part of your moving plan.
