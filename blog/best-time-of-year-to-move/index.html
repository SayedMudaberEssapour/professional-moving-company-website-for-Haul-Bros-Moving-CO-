Best Time of Year to Move | DMV Moving Guide

March 26, 2026

Compare seasons, scheduling options, and practical moving tips for the DMV.

Choosing the best time of year to move can affect your budget, scheduling options, weather conditions, traffic, and overall moving experience.

For customers moving in Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland, the ideal moving date depends on several factors, including your home size, moving distance, work schedule, school calendar, lease deadline, and budget.

At Haul Bros Moving Co LLC, we help customers move throughout Woodbridge, Dale City, Lake Ridge, Dumfries, Stafford, Fredericksburg, Manassas, Gainesville, Haymarket, Fairfax, Springfield, Alexandria, Arlington, McLean, Tysons, Reston, Ashburn, Sterling, Washington, D.C., and surrounding Maryland communities.

The short answer is:

Spring and fall are usually the best seasons for comfortable weather and flexible scheduling. Summer is best for families with school-age children, while winter may offer the most flexibility for customers focused on availability and budget.

However, the best moving season for you depends on your specific situation.

When Is the Best Month to Move?

For many customers, May, September, October, and early November offer an excellent balance between weather and availability.

The best month depends on your priorities:

  • May: Good weather before the busiest summer period
  • June: Popular with families after the school year ends
  • July: Convenient but typically one of the busiest months
  • August: Still busy, with hot weather and school preparation
  • September: Comfortable temperatures and reduced summer demand
  • October: One of the most comfortable months for moving
  • November through February: More flexibility, but greater weather concerns

A moving date in the middle of the month and during the week may provide more options than a weekend at the beginning or end of the month.

Moving in the Spring

Spring is often considered the “balanced” moving season. Temperatures are generally more comfortable than summer, while moving companies may have greater availability than during peak season.

Advantages of moving in spring

  • Comfortable temperatures for loading and unloading
  • Less extreme heat
  • Good availability for professional movers
  • More daylight than winter
  • Active housing market
  • Excellent conditions for local moves

Challenges of moving in spring

Spring weather in the DMV can change quickly. Rain can create muddy walkways, wet boxes, and slippery surfaces. Pollen may also be a concern for customers with seasonal allergies.

If you move during spring, keep plastic covers, towels, and floor protection available. A professional moving crew can help protect your furniture and home from rain and dirt.

Moving in the Summer

Summer is the busiest moving season in many parts of the United States, including Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland.

Many families choose summer because children are out of school and parents can coordinate the move around summer vacation. The longer daylight hours can also make it easier to complete a move.

Advantages of moving in summer

  • Children do not need to miss school
  • More daylight for loading and unloading
  • Easier transition between school years
  • More predictable weather
  • Convenient for families relocating for work

Challenges of moving in summer

  • Higher demand for moving dates
  • Hot and humid weather
  • More traffic throughout the DMV
  • Popular weekends may book quickly
  • More competition for apartment elevators and loading areas

If you are planning a summer move, contact your movers as early as possible. Customers who can choose a weekday or mid-month date may have more flexibility.

Starting earlier in the morning can also make a summer move more comfortable. Morning appointments generally avoid the hottest part of the day and may reduce traffic-related delays.

Moving in the Fall

Fall is one of the strongest options for customers who want comfortable temperatures and better availability.

After summer ends, moving demand often becomes less intense. This can make it easier to find a date that fits your schedule.

Advantages of moving in fall

  • Cooler temperatures
  • Comfortable conditions for physical work
  • Less summer competition
  • Good availability for weekday moves
  • Easier loading and unloading
  • Lower risk of extreme summer heat

September and October are especially popular with customers who want a comfortable local move without the pressure of the summer season.

The main concern is that school is in session. Families should coordinate carefully with school start dates, extracurricular activities, and transportation arrangements.

Moving in the Winter

Winter may be a good option for customers with flexible schedules. The number of people moving is often lower, which can make preferred dates and arrival windows easier to find.

Advantages of moving in winter

  • More scheduling flexibility
  • Fewer competing moving appointments
  • Less summer heat and humidity
  • Easier access to some apartment loading areas
  • Good option for customers with flexible leases

Winter moving challenges

  • Snow and ice
  • Cold temperatures
  • Shorter daylight hours
  • Wet or salted walkways
  • Possible weather-related delays
  • Extra care required for sensitive items

If you are moving during winter, keep sidewalks, driveways, stairs, and entrances clear. Protect floors from snow, salt, and moisture, and allow additional travel time when weather conditions are poor.

What Is the Cheapest Time of Year to Move?

The cost of a move depends on the number of movers, moving hours, distance, furniture, stairs, elevators, parking, packing needs, and other logistical factors.

However, customers may find more scheduling flexibility during winter, certain spring dates, and fall weekdays. Moving during the middle of the month may also help avoid the heavy demand associated with lease turnover.

To control your moving costs:

  • Choose a weekday when possible.
  • Avoid the first and last days of the month.
  • Book your moving company early.
  • Declutter before moving day.
  • Pack smaller items in advance.
  • Reserve elevators and parking spaces.
  • Provide an accurate inventory.
  • Ask for a written estimate.
  • Confirm what services and equipment are included.

Is It Better to Move on a Weekday or Weekend?

Weekends are convenient because many customers are off work, but they are also among the most requested moving dates.

A weekday move may offer:

  • Better date availability
  • Less competition for elevators and loading areas
  • Lighter traffic in some areas
  • More flexibility when selecting an arrival window
  • A less crowded moving schedule

Monday through Thursday can be excellent options for customers who can take time off work or arrange a flexible schedule.

Is the Beginning or End of the Month a Good Time to Move?

The beginning and end of the month are often the busiest moving periods because many leases begin or end during those dates.

If your schedule allows, consider moving between the 5th and 25th of the month. Mid-month moving dates may offer more availability and less pressure.

If your lease requires a first-of-the-month move, book early and confirm your building’s elevator, loading dock, parking, and move-in rules.

Moving with Children

Summer is often convenient for families because children are out of school. However, it can also be the busiest and most expensive period for moving.

If you are moving during summer, consider:

  • Moving immediately after school ends
  • Moving in early August before school resumes
  • Choosing a weekday
  • Preparing children for the new neighborhood
  • Keeping familiar belongings accessible
  • Packing a separate first-night bag

Families moving during the school year should contact the new school early and plan transportation, records, and enrollment requirements ahead of time.

Moving During Extreme Heat

Summer temperatures in Northern Virginia, D.C., and Maryland can become hot and humid. Moving heavy furniture in extreme heat requires planning.

To make a hot-weather move easier:

  • Choose an early arrival window.
  • Keep water available for everyone.
  • Dress in lightweight clothing.
  • Keep pets in a cool, safe area.
  • Avoid leaving electronics or medications in hot vehicles.
  • Make sure walkways are clear.
  • Let your movers know about stairs and long walks.

Starting early is especially useful for townhouse moves, apartment moves, and homes with multiple flights of stairs.

Moving During Rain, Snow, or Ice

Weather does not always cooperate with your moving schedule. If you must move during poor weather, preparation is essential.

Before the crew arrives:

  • Clear snow and ice from walkways.
  • Place towels or protective coverings near entrances.
  • Keep boxes away from wet areas.
  • Protect hardwood and carpeted floors.
  • Secure loose outdoor items.
  • Keep important documents and valuables with you.
  • Confirm whether the building has weather-related procedures.

Professional movers can help reduce weather-related risks by using moving blankets, shrink wrap, dollies, floor protection, and careful loading procedures.

Local Moves vs. Long-Distance Moves

A local move within Northern Virginia, D.C., or Maryland may provide more flexibility because travel distances are shorter. However, apartment rules, traffic, stairs, parking, and elevator reservations still affect the schedule.

Long-distance moves require additional planning because weather and traffic conditions may affect multiple areas along the route.

For long-distance moves, consider:

  • Weather across every major travel area
  • Delivery timing
  • State and building requirements
  • Fuel and travel costs
  • Storage needs
  • Packing and inventory preparation
  • Additional time for delays

Whether you are moving locally or across state lines, early planning helps prevent avoidable problems.

How Far in Advance Should You Book Movers?

For a summer weekend move, booking several weeks in advance is strongly recommended. Customers with flexible weekday or winter dates may have more options closer to the moving date.

You should contact professional movers as soon as you know:

  • Your move date
  • Pickup and delivery addresses
  • Home or apartment size
  • Number of large items
  • Number of stairs
  • Elevator requirements
  • Parking details
  • Packing or junk removal needs

The more accurate information you provide, the more accurately your moving company can plan the crew, truck, equipment, and estimated timeline.

What Should You Ask a Moving Company?

Before booking, ask:

  1. What services are included?
  2. Is equipment included?
  3. Are moving blankets and floor protection provided?
  4. How is the moving time calculated?
  5. Are there any additional fees?
  6. What is the deposit policy?
  7. What happens if the weather is severe?
  8. Is the company insured and properly registered?
  9. How many movers will arrive?
  10. What arrival window should I expect?
  11. Can the company handle stairs and elevators?
  12. Can the company move oversized items?
  13. Are packing and unpacking services available?
  14. Is junk removal available?
  15. Will I receive written confirmation?

30 Frequently Asked Questions About the Best Time to Move

1. What is the best season to move?

Spring and fall are often the best seasons because temperatures are comfortable and moving demand may be lower than during summer. The best season depends on your schedule, budget, and weather preferences.

2. What is the cheapest month to move?

Winter and certain weekday dates during spring or fall may offer greater availability. Moving costs depend on the move size, distance, labor time, stairs, parking, and services required.

3. Is summer a bad time to move?

No. Summer is convenient for families with children, but it is also a busy season. Book early if you plan to move during June, July, or August.

4. What is the busiest month for moving?

June, July, and August are commonly among the busiest months because many families move during summer break.

5. Is it better to move in spring or fall?

Both are good options. Spring may offer more daylight and active housing availability, while fall usually provides cooler temperatures and comfortable working conditions.

6. Is winter a good time to move?

Winter can be a good time for flexible customers who want more date options. Snow, ice, freezing temperatures, and shorter daylight hours require additional preparation.

7. Is it cheaper to move during the week?

Weekday moves may provide more scheduling flexibility and less competition than weekends. Pricing depends on the moving company and the details of the move.

8. What is the best day of the week to move?

Monday through Thursday may offer better availability than Friday or the weekend. The best day is the one that fits your lease, work schedule, and building requirements.

9. Is it cheaper to move at the beginning or end of the month?

The beginning and end of the month are often busy because of lease turnover. Mid-month dates may provide more flexibility.

10. What is the best time of day to move?

Morning arrival windows are often helpful, especially during the summer. Starting earlier can provide cooler temperatures, better daylight, and more time to complete the move.

11. How early should I book movers for a summer move?

Book as early as possible. Summer weekends and the first and last days of the month often fill quickly.

12. How early should I book movers for a winter move?

Winter may offer more flexibility, but booking early is still recommended so the company can reserve the right crew, truck, and equipment.

13. Should I move before or after school starts?

Moving before school starts gives children time to adjust. However, moving during the school year may be less expensive or easier to schedule depending on your circumstances.

14. What is the best month to move with children?

Late June or early August may work well for many families because these dates provide time to settle before school resumes.

15. Is October a good month to move?

Yes. October often provides comfortable temperatures and reduced summer demand, making it a popular month for local moves.

16. Is July a good month to move?

July can be convenient for families, but it is usually busy and hot. Reserve your moving company early and consider an early morning arrival.

17. Can movers work in the rain?

Movers can often work in light rain with proper preparation. Severe weather may create safety concerns and could require schedule adjustments.

18. Can movers work in snow?

Moves may be possible during light snow if walkways and roads are safe. Heavy snow and ice can create dangerous conditions and delays.

19. How do I protect my floors during a rainy move?

Use floor protection, towels, runners, and clear paths. Professional movers may provide protective materials as part of their equipment.

20. Is it better to move before or after closing on a home?

You should move after the home is legally available for occupancy and the closing process is complete. Confirm access with your real estate professional or closing attorney.

21. What is the best time to move into an apartment?

The best time depends on elevator availability, loading-area rules, and your lease. Weekdays and mid-month dates may provide more options.

22. Should I reserve an apartment elevator?

Yes. Many apartment buildings require elevator reservations for moving. Confirm the rules with the leasing office before booking your movers.

23. What is the best time to move in Northern Virginia?

Spring and fall are often comfortable seasons for Northern Virginia moves. Summer is popular but busy, and winter requires preparation for snow and ice.

24. What is the best time to move in Washington, D.C.?

Weekdays and mid-month dates may help reduce scheduling pressure. Traffic, parking restrictions, building rules, and loading zones should be confirmed early.

25. What is the best time to move in Maryland?

Fall and spring may offer comfortable conditions, while summer is convenient for families. Your exact city, building, and moving distance will affect planning.

26. How much notice should I give a moving company?

Contact movers as soon as you know your date and addresses. More notice is especially important for summer weekends, apartment moves, commercial moves, and long-distance relocations.

27. Should I move on a holiday weekend?

Holiday weekends may be convenient but are often busy. Confirm availability, building access, parking rules, and any holiday scheduling policies before booking.

28. Is it better to pack before the movers arrive?

Yes. Packing smaller items before moving day can help the crew focus on loading furniture and large belongings. Professional packing services may also be available.

29. What information should I provide when requesting a moving estimate?

Provide your pickup and delivery addresses, home size, moving date, stairs, elevators, parking information, large items, number of boxes, and any packing or junk removal needs.

30. How do I choose the best moving company?

Choose a company that provides clear estimates, professional communication, proper equipment, reliable scheduling, transparent policies, and positive customer reviews. Always ask what is included and whether additional fees apply.
