1. Where Is Manassas, Virginia?

Manassas is located in Northern Virginia, southwest of Washington, DC.

The independent City of Manassas is surrounded by Prince William County, while the neighboring City of Manassas Park is a separate independent city.

Many properties outside Manassas City limits still use a Manassas mailing address.

Nearby Communities

Manassas Park: A separate city adjacent to Manassas, with its own local government and school division.

Bristow: A residential area west of Manassas featuring established neighborhoods and newer housing developments.

Gainesville: Located farther west, with shopping destinations, detached homes, and townhouse communities.

Haymarket: Offers residential communities and access to the Interstate 66 corridor.

Centreville: Located east of Manassas in Fairfax County, providing connections toward Fairfax and Washington, DC.

Woodbridge: Located southeast of Manassas, with residential neighborhoods and access to Interstate 95.

Why the Exact Address Matters

Two homes with Manassas mailing addresses may fall under different school systems, tax authorities, and public-service providers.

Before purchasing or renting, verify the jurisdiction using the property's official address and parcel information.

Manassas offers a variety of residential environments, from historic neighborhoods to newer suburban communities.

4. How Much Does It Cost to Rent in Manassas?

Manassas offers rental apartments, townhouses, condominiums, and detached homes.

5. Cost of Living in Manassas, VA

Housing is one of the largest expenses associated with living in Manassas.

Other household expenses may include transportation, utilities, insurance, childcare, groceries, and property maintenance.

Property Taxes

Manassas City, Manassas Park, and Prince William County have separate taxing authorities.

The applicable tax rates depend on the property's jurisdiction.

A Manassas mailing address does not necessarily mean a property is taxed by the City of Manassas.

HOA and Condominium Fees

Some residential communities charge recurring association fees.

These may cover common-area maintenance, shared amenities, landscaping, or building operations.

Transportation Costs

Commuters should consider:

  • Fuel.
  • Vehicle maintenance.
  • Parking.
  • Express-lane tolls where applicable.
  • VRE fares.
  • Bus fares.

Home Maintenance

Detached homes may involve additional expenses for landscaping, exterior maintenance, HVAC systems, and repairs.

School assignments depend on the exact property location.

The City of Manassas operates its own public school division.

  • Mayfield Intermediate School.
  • Baldwin Elementary School.
  • Haydon Elementary School.

  • Weems Elementary School.

Prince William County Public Schools

Properties with Manassas mailing addresses outside city limits may be served by Prince William County Public Schools.

Manassas Park City Schools

Manassas Park operates a separate school division.

Use the relevant school division's official address-based locator or contact the school division directly.

7. Commuting From Manassas to Washington, DC, Fairfax & Arlington

Transportation is a major consideration for people moving to Manassas.

Interstate 66

Interstate 66 connects the broader Manassas area with Fairfax County, Arlington, and Washington, DC.

Traffic conditions can vary significantly during peak hours.

Route 28

Route 28 provides access toward Centreville, Chantilly, and the Dulles corridor.

Route 234

Route 234 and the Prince William Parkway connect Manassas with surrounding parts of Prince William County.

Virginia Railway Express

The VRE Manassas Line offers commuter rail service toward Alexandria, Crystal City, and Washington, DC.

Manassas Station is located at:

9451 West Street, Manassas, VA 20110.

The station provides access to commuter rail and local transportation connections.

OmniRide

OmniRide operates regional commuter and local bus services in the Prince William County area.

Routes and schedules should be checked before relying on a particular service.

Does Manassas Have a Metro Station?

Manassas does not have a Metrorail station.

Residents may use VRE, bus services, or drive to Metro stations in other communities.

Commuting Advice

Test your commute during the actual hours you expect to travel.

A route that appears convenient on a weekend may take significantly longer during weekday rush hour.
