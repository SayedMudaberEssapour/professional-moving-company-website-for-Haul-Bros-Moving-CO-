1. We Believe Moving Prices Should Be Easy to Understand

One of the biggest concerns customers have when hiring movers is pricing.

Nobody wants to receive one price before moving day and discover a completely different bill afterward.

That's why Haul Bros focuses on transparent moving rates and clearly explaining what is included.

Our standard local moving rates currently start at:

2 Movers + Moving Truck

$395 for the first 2 hours

$159 per additional hour

3 Movers + Moving Truck

$495 for the first 2 hours

$189 per additional hour

4 Movers + Moving Truck

$695 for the first 2 hours

$249 per additional hour

Additional moving time is billed in 15-minute increments, helping customers avoid paying for an unnecessary full additional hour when only a portion is used.

Our goal is simple:

Know what you're paying for before moving day begins.

2. The Moving Truck Is Included With Our Standard Full-Service Local Moves

Our professional moving crew arrives with the truck and equipment needed for the scheduled relocation.

How to Choose the Best Moving Company in Virginia

Before hiring any mover, ask these questions:

Is the company properly registered for the type of move I need?

Verify applicable credentials before booking.

Is the company insured?

Ask what insurance and valuation protection applies to your move.

Does the company have recent reviews?

Don't look only at lifetime ratings. Read recent customer experiences.

Is pricing clearly explained?

Make sure you understand the minimum, hourly rate, additional time, and other possible charges.

What is included?

Ask about:

  • Truck
  • Fuel
  • Moving blankets
  • Equipment
  • Furniture protection
  • Stairs
  • Elevators
  • Disassembly
  • Reassembly

Who is actually performing the move?

Know who you're hiring.

Does the company communicate professionally?

Communication before moving day is often a good indicator of how organized the move itself will be.
