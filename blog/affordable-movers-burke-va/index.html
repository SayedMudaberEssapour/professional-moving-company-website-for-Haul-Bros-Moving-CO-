How Can You Make a Burke Move More Affordable?

Preparation can help reduce unnecessary moving time.

Declutter First

Don't pay to move belongings you no longer want.

Donate, sell, recycle, or dispose of unwanted items before moving day.

Finish Packing Before the Movers Arrive

If you're packing yourself, boxes should be sealed and ready to move.

Label Boxes by Room

Clearly labeling boxes helps movers place them in the correct rooms at delivery.

Disclose Stairs and Access Conditions

Tell your mover about stairs, elevators, long walks, and difficult parking beforehand.

Clear Hallways

Give movers a safe, unobstructed path.

Provide an Accurate Inventory

Include all major furniture and specialty items when requesting your estimate.

Preparation can improve efficiency without sacrificing professional service.
