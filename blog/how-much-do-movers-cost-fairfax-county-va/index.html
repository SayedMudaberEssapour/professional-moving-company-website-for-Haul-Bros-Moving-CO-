1. Understanding Moving Costs in Fairfax County, Virginia

The total cost of hiring movers depends on several factors.

These include:

  • Size of your home.
  • Amount and weight of furniture.
  • Number of moving boxes.
  • Number of movers needed.
  • Distance between properties.
  • Stairs, elevators, and walking distances.
  • Furniture disassembly and reassembly.
  • Packing and unpacking requirements.
  • Specialty items.
  • Total moving time.

For local moving projects, hourly pricing can provide flexibility when the amount of work is difficult to predict precisely.

However, customers should understand the minimum charges, billing increments, and what is included before booking.

2. How Much Does It Cost to Move a One-Bedroom Apartment in Fairfax County?

One-bedroom apartment moves are common in areas such as Fairfax, Reston, Herndon, Tysons, and Vienna.

These moves may involve:

  • One bed and mattress.
  • Dresser and nightstands.
  • Sofa or loveseat.
  • Television and TV stand.
  • Dining table and chairs.
  • Desk and office chair.
  • Moving boxes.
  • Small household furniture.

Example: One-Bedroom Apartment Move

A customer is moving from a one-bedroom apartment in Reston to another apartment in Herndon.

The customer has already packed all boxes and reserved the service elevator.

With two movers and a truck, a hypothetical four-hour move would cost:

First two hours: $395 Additional two hours: $318

Estimated total: $713

What Can Increase the Cost?

  • Multiple flights of stairs.
  • Long hallways or loading distances.
  • Limited elevator access.
  • Large sectional sofas.
  • Heavy furniture.
  • Additional packing.
  • More items than originally estimated.

How to Save Money on a One-Bedroom Move

Pack boxes before moving day, reserve elevator access, clear walkways, and provide the movers with an accurate inventory.

For a smaller move, a two-mover crew may be a practical starting point.

3. How Much Does It Cost to Move a Two-Bedroom Apartment or Townhouse?

Two-bedroom moves are common throughout Springfield, Burke, Annandale, Centreville, Chantilly, and Fairfax.

A typical two-bedroom inventory might include:

  • Two beds and mattresses.
  • Dressers and nightstands.
  • Living room sofa.
  • Dining table and chairs.
  • Entertainment center.
  • Desks or office furniture.
  • Approximately 25–50 moving boxes.
  • Additional household items.

Actual inventory varies considerably.

Example: Two-Bedroom Townhouse Move

A family is relocating from Springfield to Burke.

They have a moderately furnished two-bedroom townhouse with stairs at both properties.

Assume three movers and a truck complete the move in five hours.

First two hours: $495 Additional three hours: $567

Estimated total: $1,062

Two Movers or Three Movers?

A two-mover crew may be suitable for smaller two-bedroom moves with limited furniture.

Three movers may be more efficient when the move involves:

  • Multiple floors.
  • Heavy furniture.
  • Larger inventories.
  • Long loading distances.
  • Tight scheduling requirements.

A larger crew can sometimes reduce the number of hours required, but it does not automatically guarantee a lower total cost.

4. How Much Does It Cost to Move a Three-Bedroom House in Fairfax County?

Three-bedroom homes often contain substantially more furniture and household belongings than apartments.

These moves may involve:

  • Three bedroom sets.
  • Multiple dressers.
  • Sectional sofas.
  • Dining room furniture.
  • Office furniture.
  • Garage contents.
  • Basement furniture.
  • Outdoor furniture.
  • Dozens of moving boxes.

Example: Three-Bedroom Home Move

A family is relocating from a three-bedroom house in Centreville to another home in Chantilly.

The household is already packed, and the crew is responsible for furniture protection, loading, transportation, and unloading.

Assume a three-mover crew completes the job in seven hours.

First two hours: $495 Additional five hours: $945

Estimated total: $1,440

How Long Does a Three-Bedroom Move Take?

For planning purposes, a moderately furnished three-bedroom move might require five to eight hours with an appropriate crew.

However, heavily furnished homes, long distances, multiple truckloads, and difficult access can extend the timeline.

A pre-move assessment is recommended for larger inventories.

5. How Much Does It Cost to Move a Four-Bedroom House in Fairfax County?

Four-bedroom home relocations are generally more complex because of the amount of furniture, boxes, and household belongings involved.

Larger homes in areas such as McLean, Great Falls, Vienna, Fairfax Station, and Chantilly may require additional planning.

Common items include:

  • Four bedroom sets.
  • Multiple sofas and sectionals.
  • Large dining tables.
  • Home office furniture.
  • Basement furniture.
  • Exercise equipment.
  • Garage storage.
  • Patio furniture.
  • Large televisions.
  • Numerous moving boxes.

Example: Four-Bedroom Home Move

A family is moving from Chantilly to Centreville.

Assume the inventory fits the planned truck arrangement and four movers complete the relocation in eight hours.

First two hours: $695 Additional six hours: $1,494

Estimated total: $2,189

What If the Move Requires More Than One Truckload?

A heavily furnished four-bedroom home may exceed the capacity of a single 26-foot moving truck.

In that situation, the company may need to arrange an additional trip, truck, or crew.

This can increase the total duration and cost.

For larger homes, Haul Bros Moving Co LLC recommends a detailed inventory review or pre-move inspection before finalizing the moving plan.

7. What Factors Can Increase Your Moving Costs?

Even homes with the same number of bedrooms can have very different moving costs.

Amount of Furniture

A minimally furnished three-bedroom home may take less time than a heavily furnished two-bedroom apartment.

Stairs and Elevators

Stairs, elevator reservations, and long hallways can increase the total time required.

Travel Distance

Moving from Fairfax to Burke is different from moving from Fairfax to Fredericksburg or another state.

Packing Requirements

Packing fragile items, kitchen supplies, artwork, and household belongings requires additional labor and materials.

Specialty Items

Pianos, large safes, marble furniture, oversized appliances, and other heavy items may require specialized handling.

Truck Capacity

Large inventories may require additional trips or equipment.

Preparation

Unpacked boxes, blocked hallways, and unavailable elevators can delay the move.

8. How to Reduce Moving Costs in Fairfax County

Customers often ask:

"How can I make my move cheaper without hiring an unreliable moving company?"

Here are practical ways to reduce unnecessary expenses.

Declutter Before Moving

Donate, sell, recycle, or discard items you no longer need.

Pack Early

Have boxes sealed and labeled before your movers arrive.

Reserve Elevators

Coordinate elevator reservations and loading dock access with your building.

Prepare Furniture

Remove loose items from drawers and clear surfaces before moving day.

Choose an Appropriate Crew

Ask your moving company whether two, three, or four movers are best suited for your inventory.

Schedule in Advance

Booking early can provide more scheduling flexibility.

Ask About Discounts

Haul Bros Moving Co LLC offers eligible customers:

  • 5% military discount.
  • 5% senior discount.
  • 5% student discount.

Confirm discount eligibility and application before booking.

12. Frequently Asked Questions About Moving Costs in Fairfax County, VA

How much does it cost to move a one-bedroom apartment in Fairfax County?

Using Haul Bros' standard two-mover-and-truck pricing, a hypothetical three-to-five-hour move would cost approximately $554–$872. Actual costs depend on inventory, access, and travel.

How much does it cost to move a two-bedroom townhouse?

A four-to-six-hour move using Haul Bros' standard three-mover-and-truck pricing would calculate to approximately $873–$1,251. A detailed inventory is needed for a personalized quote.

How much does it cost to move a three-bedroom house?

A hypothetical five-to-eight-hour move with three movers and a truck would calculate to approximately $1,062–$1,629 under the listed standard rates. Larger inventories may require more time or resources.

How much does it cost to move a four-bedroom house?

A six-to-nine-hour move using the standard four-mover-and-truck rates would calculate to approximately $1,691–$2,438. Large inventories, additional truckloads, or specialized handling can change the total.

Is it cheaper to hire two movers instead of three?

Not necessarily. Two movers have a lower crew rate, but a three-mover team may finish some jobs faster. Compare estimated total costs for both options.

Do movers charge extra for stairs in Fairfax County?

Policies vary. Haul Bros does not charge a separate standard stair fee, although stairs can increase the total time required for an hourly move.

Are moving blankets and shrink wrap included?

Haul Bros includes standard furniture blankets, shrink wrap, and moving equipment with applicable moving services.

How much does labor-only moving cost?

Haul Bros offers a standard two-mover labor-only package starting at $295 for the first two hours, with additional time at $125 per hour.

Is a moving truck included in the price?

Yes, for Haul Bros' moving crew and truck packages. Labor-only service does not include transportation.

Can I get a flat-rate moving quote?

Custom flat-rate estimates may be available for qualifying moves after reviewing inventory, travel, and access requirements.

How much should I budget for packing services?

Packing costs depend on the number of rooms, volume of belongings, materials required, and labor. Request a separate packing estimate for an accurate amount.

Do moving companies require a deposit?

Haul Bros generally requires a $100 reservation deposit applied toward the final moving balance. Review the applicable reservation and refund terms before paying.

Do movers offer military discounts?

Haul Bros offers a 5% discount to eligible military customers, subject to confirmation when booking.

What is the best way to get an accurate moving estimate?

Provide a complete furniture inventory, pickup and delivery locations, building access information, moving date, and any packing or specialty-item requirements.

How far in advance should I book movers in Fairfax County?

Two to four weeks is a useful target for many local moves. Larger relocations and busy moving periods may require more advance planning.

What is the most affordable way to move locally?

Decluttering, packing in advance, providing an accurate inventory, and choosing an appropriately sized crew can help reduce unnecessary moving time and expenses.

Does Haul Bros serve all of Fairfax County?

Haul Bros serves communities throughout Fairfax County, including Fairfax, Chantilly, Centreville, Burke, Springfield, Annandale, Vienna, Tysons, Reston, Herndon, McLean, and Lorton, subject to scheduling and project requirements.
