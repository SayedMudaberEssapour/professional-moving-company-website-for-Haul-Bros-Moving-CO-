How to Get the Best Price on Professional Movers in Virginia: 17 Money-Saving Tips

By Haul Bros Moving Co LLCUpdated September 22, 202615 minute read

Discover practical ways to save money when hiring professional movers in Virginia without sacrificing careful handling, clear pricing, or qualified service.

Quick answer: How can I save money when hiring movers in Virginia?

Obtain several itemized written estimates, provide an accurate inventory, declutter before moving, compare total costs rather than hourly rates, choose the right crew, and ask about minimum hours and additional fees. If your schedule is flexible, request prices for different dates and verify the mover’s authority and insurance.

1. Understand how moving companies calculate their prices

Compare the hourly or flat rate, crew size, minimum hours, truck use, travel time, fuel, equipment, stairs, elevators, packing, specialty items, payment terms, and what could change the final price. Ask every company for the total cost for your specific move.

2. Know what professional movers cost in Virginia

Virginia moving prices vary by home size, distance, inventory, access, and services. Online averages are useful for a preliminary budget, but a written estimate based on your addresses and inventory is more meaningful.

3. Get at least three comparable moving quotes

Give each mover the same addresses, date, home details, inventory, box count, access conditions, specialty items, parking information, and requested services. Ask for itemized estimates in writing.

4. Don’t choose movers based only on the hourly rate

A higher hourly rate can still produce a lower total when a larger or better-equipped crew finishes sooner and truck or fuel charges are included. Compare total estimates and assumptions.

5. Choose the right number of movers

Two movers may suit a small, straightforward move; three or four may reduce total time for a larger home or difficult access. Ask for total-cost estimates for each suitable crew configuration.

6. Declutter before your final estimate

Sell, donate, recycle, or responsibly dispose of items you do not want to transport. Tell the mover when inventory changes so the estimate can be updated.

7. Pack your own belongings selectively

Packing clothing, linens, books, and nonfragile items yourself may reduce labor. Consider professional packing for artwork, glassware, antiques, and complicated electronics.

8. Get moving boxes without overspending

Reuse clean sturdy boxes, choose smaller boxes for heavy items, use specialty cartons for fragile belongings, and label every box by destination room.

9. Prepare your home before movers arrive

Finish packing, seal and label boxes, separate nonmoving items, clear paths, reserve elevators and parking, and arrange access at both properties.

10. Ask whether another moving date costs less

Ask about alternate dates when your schedule allows. Do not assume a particular weekday, season, or month is always cheaper because pricing and availability vary.

11. Reserve elevators and parking in advance

Confirm freight elevators, loading docks, moving hours, parking, certificates of insurance, deposits, and legal unloading locations with both building managers.

12. Avoid unnecessary storage

Compare a direct move with the cost of storage, second-stage transportation, loading, and unloading. A low monthly unit price is not automatically the lowest total.

13. Ask about discounts before booking

Haul Bros’ stated military, senior, and student discounts are 5% for eligible customers. Confirm eligibility, qualifying charges, and whether discounts combine before booking.

14. Understand travel time, minimum hours, and extra fees

Ask when billing begins and ends, the minimum, how extra time is billed, and whether truck use, fuel, travel, tolls, stairs, elevators, packing, parking, or specialty items are included.

15. Compare professional movers with DIY moving fairly

Include truck rental, mileage, fuel, equipment, supplies, your time, loading help, driving, and damage responsibility when comparing DIY with professional service. Labor-only service may suit customers who already have transportation.

16. Verify authority and insurance

For Virginia intrastate moves, check the applicable Virginia DMV authority. For interstate moves, verify FMCSA household-goods authority, registration, insurance, and complaint information. A USDOT number alone does not prove active authority.

17. Read the estimate and cancellation policy

Before paying a deposit, confirm the deposit, refund terms, cancellation deadline, moving date, arrival window, crew, services, charges, and how the final price can change. Never sign blank documents.

Frequently Asked Questions: How to Save Money on Movers in Virginia

What is the cheapest way to hire professional movers in Virginia?

Reduce your inventory, prepare before arrival, compare complete written estimates, and choose a crew suited to the job.

What day of the week is cheapest to hire movers?

There is no universally cheapest day. Ask about alternate dates because pricing and availability vary by company.

Can I save money by packing myself?

You may reduce packing labor and materials, but use sturdy boxes and consider professional packing for fragile or valuable items.

How can I avoid hidden moving fees?

Request an itemized estimate covering labor, minimums, truck use, fuel, travel, stairs, elevators, packing, specialty items, parking, and changes.

How do I verify a moving company in Virginia?

Check Virginia DMV authority for intrastate work and FMCSA registration, household-goods authority, and insurance for interstate moves.

What is the best way to get an accurate estimate?

Provide both addresses, a complete inventory, box count, access details, moving date, and specialty items, then request a written estimate.
