How Can You Save Money on a Lorton Move?

There are several practical ways to make your move more efficient.

Declutter Before Moving

Donate, sell, recycle, or dispose of items you don't want transported.

Finish Packing Before the Crew Arrives

If you're handling your own packing, have boxes sealed and labeled before moving day.

Label Boxes by Room

This helps movers place boxes directly into the correct rooms at your new home.

Reserve Elevators Early

Don't wait until moving day to discover that the elevator isn't available.

Clear Hallways and Entrances

Make sure your moving crew has a clear path.

Provide an Accurate Inventory

Tell your mover about all large furniture and specialty items beforehand.

Preparation can reduce unnecessary moving time.
