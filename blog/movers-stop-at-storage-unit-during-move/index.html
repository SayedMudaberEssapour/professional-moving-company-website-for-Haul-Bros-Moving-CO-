A storage stop may be useful when you’re moving belongings between a home and a unit, picking up items from storage on the way to a new home, or splitting a move between your residence and storage. Planning the stop in advance helps the moving company account for it in the estimate and schedule.

What to Share When Requesting a Multi-Stop Move

Provide these details before booking:

  • The pickup, storage, and final delivery addresses
  • Which items are being picked up or dropped off at each location
  • The storage unit size and floor or drive-up access
  • Gate codes, entry instructions, and access hours
  • Whether facility staff require advance notice or a reservation
  • Stairs, elevators, loading areas, and truck-parking rules
  • Any large, fragile, or heavy items

If the storage facility has a keypad, Bluetooth access, or another entry system, make sure the crew has the required instructions before arrival. Never send access credentials through an unsecured channel if the facility has a safer method.

How a Storage Stop Can Affect the Move

A storage stop adds another location to the route. The time and estimate may depend on the distance between addresses, the belongings being handled, facility access, and the time required to load or unload.

Tell your mover about every stop before the move date. Adding a stop on moving day may affect the schedule and cost, and the company will need to confirm whether it can accommodate the change.

How to Prepare for Moving Day

Use our moving-day preparation checklist alongside the storage-specific steps below.

  • Confirm the storage facility’s access hours and rules.
  • Make sure the unit is available and accessible on the scheduled date.
  • Identify which belongings go to the home and which go to storage.
  • Label boxes by destination where possible.
  • Keep essentials and valuables with you.
  • Tell the moving company if the inventory or addresses change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can movers pick up items from storage and deliver them to my home?

Haul Bros can discuss moving belongings from storage as part of your plan. Provide the storage address, access details, inventory, and delivery address so the team can confirm availability and pricing.

Can movers take some belongings to storage and the rest to my new home?

A multi-stop plan can be discussed when you request your estimate. Tell Haul Bros which items go to each location and share the addresses and access requirements.

Do I need to be present at the storage unit?

Ask the facility about its access rules and confirm with Haul Bros what access arrangements the crew will need. Provide any required entry instructions before moving day.

Will a storage stop change the moving estimate?

It may. The route, loading and unloading work, facility access, and requested services can affect the estimate. Include the stop when requesting a quote and ask for the plan in writing.

Can Haul Bros store my belongings until my new home is ready?

Yes. Haul Bros offers storage solutions and can coordinate delivery when your destination is ready. Confirm facility arrangements, fees, access terms, and timing for your move.
