Yes, Haul Bros Moving Co LLC is fully licensed and insured with active US DOT #4497497. We carry full general liability and cargo insurance to protect your belongings throughout every local and regional move.

What areas do you serve in Northern Virginia?

We proudly serve Woodbridge, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Manassas, Stafford, Springfield, Burke, Centreville, Fredericksburg, and the entire Washington D.C. metropolitan area.
