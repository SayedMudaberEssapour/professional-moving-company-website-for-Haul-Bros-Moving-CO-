Thinking about moving to Burke, Virginia?

Located in Fairfax County, Burke offers established residential neighborhoods, access to outdoor recreation, commuter rail service, and proximity to major employment centers throughout Northern Virginia and Washington, DC.

Whether you're relocating from Woodbridge, moving from an apartment in Alexandria, purchasing your first townhouse, or looking for a home closer to Fairfax, Burke may be worth exploring.

But before moving, you'll want to understand the local housing market, neighborhood differences, school assignments, commuting options, and cost of living.

This guide covers what prospective residents should know about moving to Burke, VA, in 2027.

Quick Answer: Is Burke, Virginia, a Good Place to Live?

Burke is worth considering for households that prefer established suburban neighborhoods, outdoor recreation, and access to Fairfax County services.

Some of Burke's most notable features include:

  • Established townhouses and single-family homes.
  • The Burke Centre planned community.
  • Fairfax County Public Schools.
  • Burke Lake Park and other recreational spaces.
  • Virginia Railway Express commuter rail access.
  • Proximity to Springfield, Fairfax, and Fairfax Station.
  • Access to major roads including Fairfax County Parkway and Burke Lake Road.

However, housing costs can be significant, and commuting times vary substantially depending on destination, route, and time of day.

Bottom line: Burke can be a strong fit for buyers and renters seeking a suburban setting, but comparing specific properties, school assignments, and actual commute times is essential.

1. Where Is Burke, VA?

Burke is an unincorporated community in Fairfax County, Virginia, southwest of Washington, DC.

It is near Springfield, Fairfax, Fairfax Station, and other established Northern Virginia communities.

Burke is primarily associated with ZIP code 22015, although postal addresses and community boundaries do not always align perfectly.

Major Roads Near Burke

Burke Lake Road: An important local connection through the Burke area.

Fairfax County Parkway (Route 286): Provides regional access to nearby employment and residential areas.

Ox Road (Route 123): Connects Burke-area residents toward Fairfax and other communities.

Braddock Road: Provides east-west access across parts of Fairfax County.

Who Might Consider Moving to Burke?

Burke may appeal to:

  • Households looking for townhouses or detached homes.
  • People working in Fairfax County.
  • Commuters who can use VRE.
  • Residents seeking access to parks and trails.
  • Military and federal employees working at regional installations or offices.
  • People relocating from busier urban neighborhoods.

2. Best Neighborhoods and Communities to Explore in Burke, VA

There is no single neighborhood that is best for everyone.

The right choice depends on housing budget, preferred property type, transportation, community rules, and lifestyle.

Burke Centre

Best for exploring planned-community amenities and a mix of housing types.

Burke Centre is one of the area's best-known planned residential communities.

It is organized into five neighborhoods:

  1. The Commons.
  2. The Landings.
  3. The Oaks.
  4. The Ponds.
  5. The Woods.

The community includes a mix of townhouses and detached homes, with shared recreational spaces and neighborhood amenities.

Buyers should review applicable homeowners association documents, assessments, parking rules, and property-specific restrictions before purchasing.

Burke Centre — The Commons

The Commons is worth exploring for buyers interested in established residential streets within the larger Burke Centre community.

Compare individual townhouse and detached-home listings rather than assuming all properties have the same layout or amenities.

Burke Centre — The Landings

The Landings is another section of Burke Centre where prospective buyers can compare home sizes, parking arrangements, and access to community amenities.

Burke Centre — The Oaks

The Oaks offers another option within Burke Centre's planned-community structure.

Pay attention to the exact property location, nearby roads, and any association requirements.

Burke Centre — The Ponds

The Ponds may appeal to people interested in Burke Centre's green spaces and community setting.

Check the location of individual homes relative to trails, roads, and community facilities.

Burke Centre — The Woods

The Woods is another established part of Burke Centre.

Review individual properties for floor plans, outdoor space, parking, and proximity to local amenities.

Lake Braddock Area

Best for exploring established neighborhoods near Lake Braddock Secondary School and Burke Lake Road.

This part of Burke includes residential neighborhoods with access to local roads, schools, and community amenities.

Some properties may have older floor plans or multilevel layouts, so buyers should evaluate renovation needs and accessibility.

Burke Station Square and Nearby Communities

Best for comparing townhouse-style housing and established residential developments.

The Burke Station Square area provides another option for people considering a townhouse or smaller residential property.

Review parking availability, association documents, property condition, and outdoor maintenance responsibilities.

Rolling Road Area

Best for households prioritizing access to the Rolling Road VRE station.

Residential areas around Rolling Road may be worth investigating for commuters who want an alternative to driving their entire route.

Train schedules and first-mile transportation should be checked before choosing a home.

3. How Much Does It Cost to Buy a Home in Burke, VA?

Housing prices are one of the most important considerations when relocating to Burke.

According to Redfin's Burke housing market data for the three months ending September 2026, the median sale price was approximately $775,000.

Compare Burke with nearby Fairfax County communities using the same reporting period and property types.

These figures describe recorded sales across different property types. They are not asking prices or predictions of what a specific home will cost in 2027.

What Types of Homes Are Available?

Townhouses

Townhouses may offer a smaller footprint than detached homes, though many have multiple levels.

Single-Family Homes

Detached properties can offer additional space and yard areas, depending on the lot and neighborhood.

Condominiums and Other Attached Housing

Availability varies. Buyers seeking lower-maintenance housing should compare individual listings and association responsibilities.

Costs Beyond the Purchase Price

Remember to budget for:

  • Mortgage payments.
  • Property taxes.
  • Homeowners insurance.
  • HOA or condominium fees, if applicable.
  • Maintenance and repairs.
  • Utilities.
  • Closing costs.
  • Moving expenses.

Are Homes in Burke Expensive?

Burke is part of the broader Northern Virginia housing market, where home prices can be substantial relative to many other parts of the country.

The appropriate budget depends on household income, financing, property size, and ownership expenses.

For current inventory and prices, compare recent closed sales with active listings in the exact neighborhood you're considering.

4. How Much Does It Cost to Rent in Burke, VA?

Renters should expect prices to vary by property type, bedroom count, condition, and lease terms.

Realtor.com's September 2026 Burke market summary reported a median advertised rent of approximately $3,399 per month.

This is a listing-market measure, not the rent paid by every Burke household.

A townhouse or detached rental home may be priced differently from a smaller apartment.

Before Signing a Lease

Ask about:

  • Security deposits.
  • Pet policies.
  • Parking assignments.
  • Utilities.
  • Lease renewal terms.
  • Move-in requirements.
  • HOA restrictions.
  • Maintenance responsibilities.

If you're moving into a condominium or managed residential community, confirm whether movers need advance approval.

5. Schools in Burke, Virginia

Schools are an important consideration for many households relocating to Burke.

Public schools in the area are administered by Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS).

However, school assignments are determined by the exact residential address.

Lake Braddock Secondary School

Lake Braddock Secondary School serves grades 7–12 and is located on Burke Lake Road.

It is one of the secondary schools associated with the broader Burke area.

Robinson Secondary School

Robinson Secondary School is located in nearby Fairfax and also serves grades 7–12.

Parts of the broader Burke and Burke Centre area are associated with the Robinson school pyramid.

Elementary Schools in and Around Burke

Examples include:

  • Terra Centre Elementary School.
  • Cherry Run Elementary School.
  • White Oaks Elementary School.
  • Fairview Elementary School.
  • Laurel Ridge Elementary School.
  • Bonnie Brae Elementary School.

Important: This list does not mean every Burke address is assigned to these schools.

How to Check Your Assigned School

Use the official Fairfax County Public Schools Boundary Locator:

https://boundary.fcps.edu/

Enter the exact residential address to review the elementary, middle, and high school assignments for the applicable school year.

School boundaries can change, so confirm assignments directly with FCPS before making a housing decision.

What About School Ratings?

Rather than relying on a single third-party ranking, review:

  • Academic programs.
  • Course offerings.
  • Student support services.
  • Transportation eligibility.
  • Extracurricular activities.
  • Official school profiles.
  • Your child's individual needs.

6. Commuting From Burke to Washington, DC, and Northern Virginia

Commuting is a major consideration for Burke residents.

Some households drive to employment centers, while others use commuter rail.

Virginia Railway Express

Burke is served by two VRE stations:

Burke Centre Station

10399 Premier Court, Burke, VA 22015.

Rolling Road Station

9016 Burke Road, Burke, VA 22015.

Both are on the VRE Manassas Line.

VRE provides commuter rail connections toward Alexandria and Washington, DC, subject to current service schedules.

Is There a Metro Station in Burke?

Burke does not have a Washington Metro rail station within the community.

Some residents travel to nearby Metro stations or use VRE instead.

Driving From Burke

Major routes include Fairfax County Parkway, Burke Lake Road, Braddock Road, and Ox Road.

Actual commute times depend on the route, destination, traffic, and departure time.

Commuting Advice

Before renting or buying, test your expected commute during your normal work hours.

A property that looks convenient on a map may have a very different travel experience during peak traffic.

Outdoor recreation is one of Burke's most recognizable lifestyle features.

  • Fishing opportunities.
  • Boating activities.
  • Picnic areas.
  • Miniature golf.
  • Disc golf.
  • Seasonal attractions.
  • Camping opportunities.

Some activities require reservations or fees.

Official park information:

https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/parks/burkelake

Burke Centre Trails and Open Spaces

Burke Centre includes shared community spaces and a trail network.

Access and amenities may depend on association rules.

Local Recreation

Residents can also explore nearby Fairfax County parks, recreation centers, sports facilities, and public programs.

For households that value outdoor activity, proximity to these amenities can be a useful factor when comparing homes.

12. Moving Into a Burke Townhouse: What You Should Know

Townhouses are common in parts of Burke and Burke Centre.

Many have multiple floors, narrower stairways, or designated parking arrangements.

Check Stair Access

Let your movers know how many flights of stairs are involved.

Measure Large Furniture

Check sectional sofas, bed frames, entertainment centers, and large cabinets against doorways and staircases.

Confirm HOA Rules

Some communities regulate moving truck parking or require notice before move-in.

Plan Furniture Disassembly

Beds and other qualifying furniture may need to be disassembled for safe handling.

Protect Floors and Walls

Appropriate furniture wrapping and floor protection can help reduce avoidable damage.

Prepare Boxes Before Arrival

Clearly labeled and sealed boxes make loading and unloading more organized.
