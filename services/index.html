Questions before you book

Get the service details clear before choosing your crew and moving date.

What information do you need for a moving estimate?

Share your date, both addresses, a room-by-room inventory, approximate box count, stairs or elevators, parking and carrying distances. Include packing, storage, extra stops and heavy or fragile items. Build a moving inventory before requesting your quote.

Can I combine moving, packing and storage?

Yes. Ask for the combination you need when requesting your estimate. Confirm which rooms are packed, the materials supplied, the storage arrangement and any later delivery. Each service and charge should appear in your written scope.

How many movers should I book?

Crew size depends on the belongings, item weight, access and available loading window. Send photos and your inventory so the team can recommend a crew. A larger crew does not guarantee a particular completion time or total price.

Can you load my rental truck or storage container?

Ask for labor-only loading or unloading. Provide the vehicle or container size, ramp or liftgate details, parking position and access deadline so the team can confirm the work and equipment.

Can you meet my building’s insurance and elevator requirements?

Send the building’s COI instructions, certificate-holder details, move hours and reservation deadlines before booking. Ask the team to confirm the documentation it can provide, and arrange building approval and access in advance.

What happens after I pay a deposit?

A standard reservation deposit is generally $100 and is credited toward the final balance. A reservation is confirmed after Haul Bros accepts the booking and confirms the deposit receipt. Review the Terms of Service and your written agreement for cancellation, rescheduling and any applicable exceptions.

Can you move a piano, safe or unusually heavy item?

Request an item-specific review before booking. Send dimensions, approximate weight, photos, and the stairs, doors and loading path at both addresses. Handling depends on safe access, available equipment and the confirmed scope.
