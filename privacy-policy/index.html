Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information and how to contact us about your privacy.

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Haul Bros Moving Co LLC (“Haul Bros,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect, and retain personal information when you visit www.haulbrosmoving.com, request an estimate, book our services, make a payment, submit a form, or communicate with us by phone, text message, or email.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information.

Information You Provide

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Pickup and delivery addresses
  • Moving date and preferred arrival window
  • Home, apartment, townhouse, office, or storage details
  • Number of bedrooms, rooms, boxes, stairs, elevators, and large items
  • Packing, moving, junk removal, or specialty-item requests
  • Billing and payment information
  • Customer-service communications
  • Reviews, testimonials, photographs, and other content you voluntarily provide
  • Information submitted through quote, contact, booking, or service forms

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, driver’s-license numbers, bank passwords, or other highly sensitive information through our website or ordinary email.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we or our service providers may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed
  • Links clicked
  • Date, time, and duration of visits
  • Referring website
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Website performance information
  • Cookie and similar technology information

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Payment processors
  • Website hosting providers
  • Scheduling and form platforms
  • Advertising and analytics providers
  • Review websites
  • Lead-generation platforms
  • Referral partners
  • Social media platforms
  • Individuals who refer you to us
  • Publicly available sources

2. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to quote and contact requests
  • Prepare moving estimates
  • Schedule and perform moving services
  • Confirm appointments and arrival windows
  • Communicate about your move
  • Send booking, deposit, payment, and service confirmations
  • Process payments and refunds
  • Coordinate crews, trucks, equipment, elevators, parking, and access requirements
  • Provide local, apartment, townhouse, senior, commercial, labor-only, packing, unpacking, specialty-item, and junk-removal services
  • Respond to questions and customer-service requests
  • Send administrative notices
  • Request reviews or referrals
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorized activity, and security incidents
  • Maintain business and accounting records
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, insurance, licensing, tax, and transportation requirements
  • Enforce our agreements and protect our rights
  • Create aggregated or de-identified information
  • Send marketing communications where legally permitted

We may also use information for purposes disclosed at the time of collection or with your consent.

3. Text Messages and Phone Communications

If you provide your phone number, we may contact you by telephone or text message regarding:

  • Estimates
  • Scheduling
  • Arrival windows
  • Crew updates
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Deposits and payments
  • Cancellations or changes
  • Customer-service matters

Marketing text messages will be sent only where legally permitted and, where required, with your consent.

You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out of marketing messages will not prevent us from sending essential service-related communications, such as appointment confirmations, arrival updates, payment information, or cancellation notices.

You may request that we stop nonessential calls or texts by contacting us.

4. Payment Information

We may accept payments through third-party providers, including Square, Stripe, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Apple Pay, or other payment providers we may use.

Payment information may be collected and processed directly by the applicable payment provider. We generally do not store complete credit-card numbers, debit-card numbers, bank-login credentials, or payment passwords on our own systems.

Payment providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the provider you use.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies.

These technologies may help us:

  • Keep the website functioning
  • Remember preferences
  • Understand website usage
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Improve website speed and usability
  • Detect fraud and security issues
  • Provide relevant advertising where permitted

You may control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

Where required by law, we will provide additional choices regarding nonessential cookies and targeted advertising technologies.

6. Analytics and Advertising

We may use analytics and advertising providers to understand website traffic, measure marketing campaigns, and improve our advertising.

These providers may receive information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser information
  • Pages viewed
  • Website interactions
  • Approximate location
  • Referral source
  • Advertising identifiers

We do not intentionally sell sensitive personal information. We may allow certain advertising or analytics providers to collect information through cookies or similar technologies, which may be considered “sharing” or “sale” under certain privacy laws.

You may limit this activity through your browser settings, available opt-out tools, or by contacting us.

7. How We Share Personal Information

We may share personal information with:

  • Moving crews and authorized workers
  • Payment processors
  • Website hosting providers
  • Scheduling and appointment platforms
  • Customer relationship management providers
  • Email, text, and telephone communication providers
  • Analytics and advertising providers
  • Insurance companies and claims administrators
  • Accounting, legal, and professional advisers
  • Government agencies and regulatory authorities
  • Law enforcement when legally required
  • Service providers assisting with business operations
  • Business partners involved in providing requested services
  • Successors in the event of a merger, sale, reorganization, financing, or transfer of business assets

We require service providers to use personal information only for authorized business purposes and to protect it appropriately.

We do not sell customer lists to unrelated companies for their independent marketing purposes without appropriate authorization.

8. Reviews, Testimonials, and Photographs

If you voluntarily submit a review, testimonial, photograph, or other content, we may publish it on our website, social media pages, advertising materials, or business listings.

We may display your first name, last initial, general service location, review, photograph, or other information you make available.

We will not knowingly publish private information that you did not authorize us to use.

If you want us to remove a testimonial, photograph, or content that we control, contact us using the information below. Removal may not be possible from third-party platforms that control their own content.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services
  • Maintain customer and transaction records
  • Process payments
  • Respond to disputes or claims
  • Comply with tax, accounting, insurance, licensing, transportation, and legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and prevent fraud
  • Enforce agreements
  • Resolve complaints

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, destroy, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to legal and operational requirements.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

However, no website, email system, text-message system, payment system, or internet transmission method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for using reasonable care when communicating sensitive information and protecting your email, device, and payment credentials.

  • Access personal information we maintain about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of personal information

  • Opt out of targeted advertising
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Opt out of certain profiling activities
  • Appeal a decision regarding a privacy request
  • Receive equal service and pricing when exercising privacy rights

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We may need to retain information required for tax, accounting, insurance, legal, security, fraud-prevention, or contractual purposes.
