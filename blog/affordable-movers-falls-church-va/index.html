How Can You Save Money on a Falls Church Move?

Preparation can help make your move more efficient.

Declutter Before Moving

Donate, sell, recycle, or dispose of items you don't want transported.

Finish Packing Before the Crew Arrives

If you're packing yourself, have boxes sealed and ready.

Label Boxes by Room

This helps movers place boxes in the correct rooms at delivery.

Reserve Elevators Early

Don't wait until moving day if your building requires a reservation.

Confirm Truck Parking

Ask your building where the moving truck can park.

Clear Pathways

Keep hallways and entrances free of obstacles.

Provide an Accurate Inventory

Include all major furniture and specialty items in your estimate request.

Preparation can reduce unnecessary moving time.
