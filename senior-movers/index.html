Senior moving & downsizing at a glance

Haul Bros helps seniors and their designated contacts plan moving, packing, room placement and storage or cleanout needs. Share the destination layout, access requirements and belongings to keep so the agreed services match the transition.

Request your estimate ↗

Choose the work you need

Your accepted estimate identifies the specific services, equipment and tasks included.

  • Moves to a smaller home or apartment
  • Moves involving senior-community access requirements
  • Packing or unpacking selected rooms by arrangement
  • Furniture placement using a destination plan
  • Storage coordination when belongings need separate handling
  • Downsizing-related removal of accepted unwanted items

Decide what goes where before loading

Separate items going to the new home, family, storage or removal. Label destinations clearly and provide a placement plan for larger furniture.

Keep medications, important documents, valuables and everyday essentials with the customer or designated contact. Identify items that should remain accessible until the move is complete.

Coordinate with the destination and family contact

Confirm moving hours, elevator reservations, entrance procedures and any COI deadline with the property. Choose an authorized contact who can answer questions and approve changes.

Request the pacing, packing and setup tasks you need in the written scope. The service plan should reflect the customer’s preferences and the access available.

What to send for your estimate

Provide the details below, then update the team if your inventory, access or dates change.

  • Moving date and designated contact
  • Pickup and destination addresses
  • Belongings to move, store or remove
  • Destination room plan and furniture fit
  • Building rules and access windows
  • Packing, unpacking and essential-item needs

Keep the plan in writing

Review the services, pricing method, arrival or delivery window, and any additional charges before booking.

Standard local hourly service has a two-hour minimum unless otherwise agreed. Billable time runs from arrival at pickup through completion of unloading and agreed work, including travel between addresses.

Packing, materials, storage, specialty items, junk removal and labor-only work require their own confirmed scope and pricing. Long-distance pricing depends on the route and applicable agreement.

Senior moving & downsizing questions

Can family members coordinate the move?

Yes. Identify the authorized contact and decision-maker before booking. Share the customer’s priorities and any building or scheduling requirements.

Can you help with downsizing?

Request moving, storage and removal tasks based on the belongings being kept or passed on. Confirm each destination and service in the estimate.

Can you unpack at the new home?

Ask for the rooms and tasks you need. Unpacking and placement beyond the agreed moving work must be included in the accepted scope.

Are senior discounts available?

Ask about current eligibility and qualifying services when estimating. Any discount and conditions should appear in your written quote.

A standard reservation deposit is generally $100 and is credited toward the final balance. Your booking is confirmed after acceptance and deposit receipt. Check the Terms of Service and your agreement for cancellation, rescheduling and any applicable exceptions.
