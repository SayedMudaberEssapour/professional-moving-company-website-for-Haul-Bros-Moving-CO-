Full-Service vs. Labor-Only Movers: What's the Difference?

By Haul Bros Moving Co LLC · September 25, 2026

Full-service or labor-only movers? Compare costs, services, trucks and packing options with Haul Bros Moving Co LLC in Northern Virginia.

Planning a move in Northern Virginia? One of the first decisions you'll make is whether to hire a moving company with a truck or hire professional movers to handle only the heavy lifting.

Both options can help make moving day easier, but they work differently.

With a crew-and-truck moving service, the moving company provides the movers and transportation. With labor-only moving, you arrange the truck, rental container, or storage space, and the moving crew handles the agreed loading or unloading work.

At Haul Bros Moving Co LLC, we offer both options because every move is different. A family relocating from a townhouse in Woodbridge to a house in Fairfax may want us to handle transportation. Someone who has already rented a U-Haul or booked a POD may only need help loading it safely.

This guide explains what each service includes, how the responsibilities differ, and how to compare the costs before booking.

Quick answer: What's the difference between full-service and labor-only movers?

Full-service moving generally means the moving company manages more of the relocation, while labor-only moving means you hire professional movers for specific physical tasks without purchasing the company's transportation service.

However, the phrase full-service doesn't have one universal package definition. Some companies use it to mean movers and a truck; others use it to describe a comprehensive service that includes packing, transportation, unloading, and unpacking.

At Haul Bros, our standard crew-and-truck service includes loading, transportation, unloading, routine furniture protection, and agreed basic furniture disassembly and reassembly. Full packing and unpacking should be specifically requested and included in your estimate.

Our labor-only service is designed for customers who already have a rental truck, portable moving container, or storage arrangement and need help with the agreed loading or unloading tasks.

1. What are full-service movers?

Full-service movers take responsibility for a broader portion of your relocation.

Depending on the package you purchase, this can include packing belongings, preparing furniture, loading the truck, transporting your shipment, unloading, and unpacking.

For customers who want a company to supply the moving truck and crew, Haul Bros offers a crew-and-truck service.

Who typically chooses a crew-and-truck move?

This service is useful for customers who don't want to rent and drive a moving truck themselves.

For example, someone moving from a two-bedroom apartment in Alexandria to a townhouse in Springfield may prefer to have one company coordinate the crew, truck, loading, transportation, and unloading.

It can also simplify planning for customers who are moving with children, have limited time, or are managing a large household inventory.

2. What are labor-only movers?

Labor-only movers provide professional moving labor without supplying the transportation component of a standard crew-and-truck move.

You might already have a rental truck, a portable storage container, or a storage unit. Instead of handling all the lifting yourself, you hire movers for the tasks you need.

At Haul Bros, labor-only work can include loading or unloading, subject to the agreed service scope.

Common labor-only moving jobs

Loading a rental truck

You arrange the rental vehicle. Our crew helps load the agreed inventory.

Loading a POD or portable container

We help load belongings into your container, with the scope and equipment confirmed in advance.

Unloading at your destination

You handle transportation and hire our crew to unload when your shipment arrives.

Storage-unit loading and unloading

Our crew assists with moving belongings into or out of a storage unit.

What are you responsible for with labor-only moving?

Unless your estimate specifies otherwise, you arrange the transportation and related logistics.

That generally means you are responsible for:

  • Renting the truck or container.
  • Paying the rental, transportation, and associated fuel charges.
  • Ensuring the truck or container is available when the movers arrive.
  • Arranging a driver and any necessary transportation between locations.
  • Coordinating access at the destination.
  • Obtaining any packing supplies or equipment not included in the agreed labor-only service.

Labor-only moving doesn't necessarily mean the crew handles every aspect of your move. Be clear about whether you need loading, unloading, furniture preparation, or additional tasks.

6. When should you choose a crew-and-truck moving service?

A crew-and-truck service may fit your needs when you want one company to handle the physical move and transportation.

Consider this option if:

  • You don't want to rent or drive a moving truck.
  • You're moving an entire apartment, townhouse, or house.
  • You need a truck and a professional crew.
  • You want the loading, drive, and unloading coordinated as one service.
  • You prefer to avoid managing a separate vehicle rental.

You have beds, a sectional sofa, dressers, a dining table, and approximately 30 boxes.

A Haul Bros crew-and-truck estimate could address the moving team, truck, loading, transportation, and unloading in one plan.

7. When should you choose labor-only movers?

  • You have a POD or another portable moving container.
  • You need a storage unit loaded or unloaded.
  • You're comfortable arranging transportation yourself.
  • You need help with heavy lifting rather than an entire relocation package.

8. What about packing and unpacking?

Packing is one of the most misunderstood parts of the term full-service moving.

Some customers expect a full-service mover to arrive, pack every cabinet and closet, move everything, and unpack the entire household.

Other customers use the same phrase simply to mean movers with a truck.

Neither interpretation should be assumed without checking the estimate.

At Haul Bros, packing and unpacking can be discussed as separate service requirements.

You can ask for:

  • Packing assistance before the move.
  • Packing for selected rooms.
  • Help preparing fragile items.
  • Unpacking assistance after delivery.

The specific services, materials, and charges should be agreed upon in writing.

For the clearest quote, tell us whether you want transportation only with moving labor, or a more comprehensive relocation that also includes packing and unpacking.

10. Who is responsible for transportation and damage?

This is an important distinction.

With a crew-and-truck move, the moving company provides the agreed transportation service.

With labor-only moving, the customer typically arranges the truck or container and transportation. Responsibility for loading, transportation, damage, and claims depends on the applicable agreements and circumstances.

Do not assume that the liability terms for a complete transportation move automatically apply in exactly the same way to a labor-only job.

Before booking, ask:

  • Who is responsible for the vehicle?
  • Who will drive it?
  • Who is responsible for securing the shipment?
  • What liability terms apply to loading and unloading?
  • What happens if an item is damaged?
  • How do I submit a claim?

Haul Bros customers can direct damage and loss claim inquiries to claims@haulbrosmoving.com.

For interstate household-goods transportation, the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration provides guidance on mover liability and valuation options. Those federal rules should not automatically be treated as the governing terms for every Virginia intrastate or labor-only job.
