Local Knowledge for a Triangle Move

Triangle, Dumfries and Quantico are different locations

Triangle is a community in Prince William County; Dumfries is a separate town, and Marine Corps Base Quantico has controlled access. Provide your full pickup and delivery addresses and building names to avoid routing or entry problems.

Route and loading considerations

US Route 1, I-95 and Fuller Road are useful local references. On moving day, road conditions and truck suitability determine the actual route. For houses, share driveway slope, stairs and parking restrictions. For apartments, reserve elevators or loading spaces and check building insurance documentation requirements.

Storage and multi-stop moves

If you are collecting belongings from a storage unit before delivery, include the facility address, gate access and all planned stops in your quote request.

Frequently Asked Questions About Triangle Movers

How much do movers cost in Triangle, VA?

Our two-mover and 26-foot truck package starts at $395 for the first two hours, then $159 for each additional hour. Three movers start at $495 and four movers at $695 for the first two hours. We provide a written estimate based on your move.

Does Haul Bros Moving serve Triangle, Virginia?

Yes. Haul Bros Moving Co LLC is based in Woodbridge, Prince William County, and serves Triangle, Dumfries, Dale City, Quantico-area civilian communities and other Northern Virginia locations, subject to availability.

Is Triangle the same as Quantico or Dumfries?

No. Triangle is a community in Prince William County near Marine Corps Base Quantico and the Town of Dumfries. Share your complete address so we can identify the correct location and access requirements.

Can you help with a military PCS move near Quantico?

We can provide moving services for military households, subject to availability, route requirements and applicable authorizations. If you need government reimbursement, weight tickets or installation access, tell us before booking so we can confirm what we can provide.

Can your moving truck enter Marine Corps Base Quantico?

Installation access is controlled by the base and cannot be assumed. Contact the installation for current entry requirements, and provide those instructions before we confirm an on-base move.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. Haul Bros offers a 5% military discount, subject to confirmation in your written estimate. We also offer 5% senior and student discounts.

How much does labor-only moving cost in Triangle?

Labor-only help with two movers starts at $295 for the first two hours, then $125 for each additional hour. This option is for customers who provide the rental truck, trailer or container.

How many movers should I book for a townhouse?

Two movers may fit smaller moves. Three movers can help with two-bedroom homes and multi-level townhouses; four movers may suit larger inventories or tight schedules. We recommend a crew after reviewing stairs, access and furniture.

Do you move apartments and condos in Triangle?

Yes. Share your floor, elevator availability, parking, loading distance and any certificate-of-insurance requirements when requesting your quote.

Do you provide packing and unpacking?

Yes. Ask about full packing, partial packing, fragile-item preparation and unpacking. Packing materials and specialty services should be listed separately in your estimate when applicable.

Can you load or unload a POD or rental truck?

Yes. Our labor-only service can help load or unload customer-provided rental trucks, trailers and portable storage containers.

Can you move items into or out of a storage unit?

Yes. Provide the facility address, unit size, gate or elevator instructions and any additional pickup or delivery stops.

Are stairs, fuel and moving equipment extra?

Our standard local moving packages do not add separate charges for ordinary stairs, elevators, fuel, truck or standard moving equipment. Your written estimate will identify any optional or specialty services.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Basic disassembly and reassembly of standard furniture is included with typical moving services. Let us know about specialty, built-in, antique or complex pieces before booking.

Do you offer office or commercial moving near Triangle?

Yes. We can quote office furniture, boxed supplies, shelving and other approved commercial inventories. Send a list of items, access conditions and preferred schedule.

How far in advance should I book movers in Triangle?

Booking several weeks ahead is helpful, particularly for weekends, month-end dates and peak summer moving periods. Contact us to check your preferred date.
