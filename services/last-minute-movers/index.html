Frequently Asked Questions About Last-Minute Moving

Can I hire movers on the same day in Northern Virginia?

Yes. Haul Bros Moving Co LLC offers same-day moving services throughout Northern Virginia, Washington, DC, and Maryland when crews and trucks are available. Contact us as early as possible to check availability.

Do you offer next-day moving services?

Yes. We can accommodate next-day moving requests depending on our schedule. We recommend contacting us with your moving details so we can confirm availability and provide an estimate.

Do you charge extra for last-minute moving?

Our standard hourly rates are transparent, and we do not automatically add a last-minute booking fee. Your estimate will identify the applicable rates and any agreed charges before booking.

Can you move an entire house on short notice?

Yes, depending on crew and truck availability. We can assist with apartments, townhouses, single-family homes, and larger residential moves. Providing an accurate inventory helps us determine the appropriate crew size and equipment.

What if my moving company cancels at the last minute?

Contact Haul Bros Moving Co LLC with your moving date, pickup address, delivery address, and approximate inventory. We'll check our availability and help you explore the fastest practical moving solution.

Can you help with just one piece of furniture?

Absolutely. We provide small-load and single-item moving services for customers who need assistance transporting furniture or other large household items.

What areas do you serve for last-minute moving?

We serve communities throughout Northern Virginia, including Woodbridge, Fairfax, Alexandria, Arlington, Springfield, Manassas, Lorton, Burke, and surrounding areas. We also provide moving services in Washington, DC, and parts of Maryland.
