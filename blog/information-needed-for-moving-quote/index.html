To prepare a useful moving estimate, a moving company needs your move date, pickup and delivery locations, a clear inventory, and access details for both homes. Packing, storage, and special handling needs can also affect the services and estimate. Sharing these details upfront helps Haul Bros Moving Co LLC plan your move in Northern Virginia.

Moving Quote Checklist

Before requesting an estimate, gather the following information:

  1. Move date and flexibility Provide your preferred date and mention whether nearby dates could work.
  2. Pickup and delivery locations Share both addresses or ZIP codes. For apartment or condo moves, include the building and unit information when available.
  3. Inventory List major furniture, appliances, and other large belongings. Include an approximate number of boxes, bags, or storage containers. Photos or a video walkthrough can help show the volume.
  4. Access at each location Tell the mover about floors, stairs, elevators, long hallways, narrow doors, long walks to the truck, loading docks, parking limits, and reservation requirements.
  5. Services you want Specify whether you need packing, unpacking, furniture disassembly or reassembly, storage, or delivery to multiple locations.
  6. Special or fragile items Identify items such as large mirrors, glass furniture, oversized televisions, pianos, safes, or other belongings that may require advance planning. Share approximate dimensions or photos when helpful.

Why These Details Matter

A quote based on incomplete information may not reflect the actual work. Inventory, packing, building access, and parking can all affect the move plan. If the details change, contact the company before moving day so the estimate and schedule can be reviewed.

For apartment and condo moves in Woodbridge, Fairfax, Alexandria, Arlington, Manassas, and nearby Northern Virginia communities, ask the property manager about elevator reservations, loading areas, moving hours, and any certificate of insurance requirements. Share the building’s instructions with your mover.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a moving quote with just my ZIP codes?

ZIP codes and a move date can help start the conversation, but the mover will usually need an inventory and access details to prepare a more specific estimate.

Do I need to list every box?

You may not know the exact box count at first. Provide your best estimate and update the moving company if the quantity changes significantly.

Should I send photos or a video walkthrough?

Photos or a video walkthrough can help show the amount and type of belongings, especially when an in-person review is not practical. Ask the company how it prefers to receive them.

Why do movers ask about stairs, elevators, and parking?

These details help the company understand access at pickup and delivery and plan the move accordingly. Building rules may also affect the schedule.

What if my inventory changes after I receive an estimate?

Contact your moving company as soon as possible. Additional items or services may change the estimated plan, so ask for the updated details in writing.
