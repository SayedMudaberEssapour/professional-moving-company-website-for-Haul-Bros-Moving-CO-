Do I Need to Reserve an Elevator for Moving Day?

By Haul Bros Moving Co LLC · November 7, 2026

Moving into or out of a Northern Virginia apartment? Learn when to reserve an elevator and what to confirm with your building and movers.

If you’re moving into or out of an apartment or condo, check with the property manager about elevator reservations before moving day. Some buildings require a reservation, limit moving hours, or designate a service elevator. Rules vary by property, so confirm the requirements for both the pickup and delivery locations.

For apartment moves in Woodbridge, Fairfax, Alexandria, Arlington, Manassas, and other Northern Virginia communities, sharing the building’s access rules with your moving company helps the crew plan the move.

What Should I Ask My Building?

Contact the leasing office, property manager, or building staff and ask:

  • Do I need to reserve an elevator?
  • Is there a designated service or freight elevator?
  • What are the available moving hours?
  • Is there a loading dock or designated truck parking area?
  • Are there rules for protecting hallways and common areas?
  • Does the building require a certificate of insurance (COI)?
  • When must documents or reservations be submitted?

Ask whether these rules apply to both move-out and move-in. If you’re moving between two managed buildings, check with both properties.

When Should I Reserve the Elevator?

Reserve as soon as you know your move date and the building allows reservations. Popular dates and time slots may fill up. Confirm the reservation details in writing, including the date, time window, elevator, loading area, and any required documents.

If your moving date changes, contact the building promptly to update or release the reservation.

What Information Should I Give My Movers?

Once you have the building’s instructions, share them with your moving company. Provide:

  • The building address and apartment number
  • Floor number and elevator type
  • Reservation date and time window
  • Loading dock or truck parking instructions
  • Any access codes or check-in steps
  • COI requirements and submission deadline
  • Rules about stairs, hallways, or service entrances

Haul Bros Moving Co LLC plans apartment moves around access details such as stairs, elevator reservations, hallway distances, and parking at both buildings. Provide the requirements when requesting an estimate so they can be considered in the move plan.

What If My Building Does Not Require a Reservation?

Even if a reservation is not required, ask about moving hours, service entrances, parking, and hallway protection. Let your moving company know how the crew will access the apartment and where the truck can park.

Apartment Moving in Northern Virginia

Buildings in Woodbridge, Fairfax, Alexandria, Arlington, Manassas, and nearby areas may have different elevator, loading dock, and parking policies. Always confirm directly with your property manager rather than relying on another resident’s experience or a previous move.

Haul Bros Moving Co LLC is based in Woodbridge and serves Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland. The team can review building access details as part of planning your move.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all apartment buildings require elevator reservations?

No. Requirements vary by building. Contact the property manager to confirm whether a reservation is needed and what moving hours are allowed.

Should I reserve the elevator at both locations?

Check with both properties. The pickup and delivery buildings may have different reservation procedures and requirements.

What if the elevator reservation is unavailable on my moving date?

Ask the property manager about other available time slots or moving procedures. Contact your moving company to discuss how the updated access schedule may affect your move.

Does my building need a certificate of insurance from the movers?

Some buildings request a COI, while others do not. Ask the property manager for the exact requirements, certificate holder information, and submission deadline, then share them with your moving company.

Can movers work around a loading dock reservation?

Provide the dock’s reservation time and access instructions when booking. Your moving company can review those details and discuss whether the schedule fits the move plan.
