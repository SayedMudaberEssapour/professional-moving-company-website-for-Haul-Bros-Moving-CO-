Long-Distance Moving Services

Long-Distance Moving Services

Move farther with a team you can trust. We help customers relocate from Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., Maryland, and surrounding areas based on route, availability, and moving requirements.

Long-Distance Moving Services From the DMV

We assist with interstate apartment, townhouse, household, military, student, office, storage-to-home, home-to-storage, small, and specialty-item moves. Contact us with pickup and delivery locations for an accurate estimate.

Our Long-Distance Services

  • Residential home moves
  • Apartment and townhouse moves
  • Small long-distance moves
  • Military and family relocations
  • Student relocations
  • Office and commercial moves
  • Packing and unpacking
  • Storage moves and solutions
  • Specialty-item transportation

Careful Planning and Protection

We review addresses, dates, distance, home size, crew, stairs, elevators, parking, specialty items, packing, storage, furniture assembly, and destination access. Services may include blankets, shrink wrap, furniture padding, dollies, floor protection, organized loading, and secure unloading.

Packing and Storage Support

We can pack entire homes, selected rooms, fragile items, kitchens, offices, artwork, electronics, and storage contents. Storage can help when homes are not ready, leases do not align, or you need temporary housing.

Long-Distance Moving FAQs

What states do you move to?

Availability depends on route, schedule, origin, destination, and move details.

Can you move a small number of items?

We may assist with smaller moves, furniture transportation, storage units, and selected large items.

Do you provide packing and storage?

Yes. Packing, unpacking, storage moves, and delivery coordination are available.

Do you offer military and student discounts?

Eligible military families and students may receive a 5% discount on qualifying services.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as possible because long-distance moves require route and schedule planning.
