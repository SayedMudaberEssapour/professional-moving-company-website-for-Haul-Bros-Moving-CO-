What Actually Makes a Moving Company "The Best"

A nice website and a cheap-sounding quote aren't enough. Before you book, look for:

  • A clear, written estimate — not a vague number over the phone
  • Professional, responsive communication
  • Proper licensing and insurance information
  • Experienced crews who've handled your type of move before
  • Real furniture and floor protection, not an afterthought
  • The right truck and equipment for your inventory
  • Honest, specific service descriptions (not just "we do everything")
  • Reviews from actual customers
  • A process for handling stairs, elevators, parking, and awkward items

A good mover asks *you* questions too — about your addresses, room count, large furniture, stairs, elevators, parking, and moving date — before locking in a final price.

Why Northern Virginia Moves Take Extra Planning

Northern Virginia isn't one uniform place to move through. It's high-rises in Arlington, narrow townhome staircases in Ashburn, gated HOA communities in Fairfax, and everything in between. A move here can involve:

  • Elevator reservations and loading docks
  • Parking restrictions and HOA rules
  • Traffic bottlenecks near I-95, I-66, Route 7, Route 28, and the Capital Beltway
  • Narrow hallways, tight staircases, or long walks from the truck to your door
  • Basement, garage, or attic items
  • Certificates of insurance required by property managers

The more of this you flag up front, the more accurately your movers can plan the right crew size, truck, and timeline — and the fewer surprises you'll hit on moving day.

Questions to Ask Before You Book Any Mover

  • Are they properly licensed and insured?
  • Does the estimate spell out exactly what's included?
  • Did they ask about stairs, elevators, parking, and walking distance?
  • Is the crew size right for your inventory?
  • Do they offer packing, storage, or labor-only options if you need them?
  • Can you find real reviews from past customers?
