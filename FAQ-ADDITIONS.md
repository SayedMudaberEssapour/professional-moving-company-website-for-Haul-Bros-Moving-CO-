## 41. What information should I send for an accurate moving estimate?

Send both addresses, your preferred date, a room-by-room inventory, photos of bulky items, and details about stairs, elevators, parking, and walking distance. Include storage units, extra stops, packing needs, and anything unusually heavy. Haul Bros can use that information to plan the crew and equipment. Review the scope and charges in your written estimate before booking.

## 42. Can I get a moving estimate from photos or a video walkthrough?

Photos or a video walkthrough can help show your inventory and access conditions. Include closets, cabinets, storage areas, and the route between your door and truck parking. Ask Haul Bros which estimate method is appropriate for your move; a complex inventory or difficult access may need a closer review. An estimate should identify what information it is based on.

## 43. How do I choose between two, three, or four movers?

Crew size depends on your inventory, access, heavy items, and the time available. A larger crew may help with multiple floors or a short elevator reservation, but it does not automatically mean a lower total price. Ask Haul Bros to explain the recommended crew and expected work so you can compare options for the same scope.

## 44. Is it usually cheaper to move on a weekday or in the middle of the month?

Weekdays and mid-month dates may offer more scheduling options than popular weekends or month-end dates. A lower price is not guaranteed. Request estimates for the dates you can use, and compare the same crew, services, and expected charges. Confirm any date-dependent pricing in writing before changing your plans.

## 45. Does my moving estimate include travel time between the two homes?

Ask your mover to explain when billable time starts and stops and how travel is calculated. Loading, driving, unloading, and any trip to or from the company base may be treated differently under the estimate. For a Northern Virginia move, traffic can affect elapsed time; review the written travel terms rather than assuming driving is free.

## 46. Can I add a storage-unit stop or a second delivery address?

Request additional stops before booking and give the address, inventory, access hours, and items for each stop. Extra loading, unloading, and travel can change the schedule and estimate. Label belongings by destination so the crew can load them in a practical order. Haul Bros can review whether the proposed stops fit the route and available service.

## 47. Do I need to pay a deposit to reserve my moving date?

Confirm the current reservation requirements directly with Haul Bros before paying. Ask whether a deposit is required, how it applies to the final bill, and what happens if you cancel or reschedule. Keep the written terms and receipt. An estimate request alone should not be treated as a confirmed moving appointment.

## 48. What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule my move?

Notify Haul Bros as soon as your plans change. Ask how the booking terms handle notice, deposits, rescheduling charges, and availability on the replacement date. These details should be confirmed before booking. If your building has reserved an elevator or loading dock, update those arrangements separately so the new date works at both addresses.

## 49. How long will my move take if I have an elevator or several flights of stairs?

The number of rooms alone cannot determine the duration. Elevator wait times, reservation limits, stairs, box count, furniture assembly, and distance to parking all affect the work. Share those details with Haul Bros for a realistic estimate. Allow extra flexibility if the building controls access or the elevator must be shared.

## 50. Can movers relocate just a couch, a bedroom set, or a few bulky items?

Ask about a small-move estimate and provide dimensions, photos, pickup and delivery addresses, and access details. A short inventory can still require a truck, minimum service time, or extra handling for stairs. Haul Bros can review the job and explain the applicable scope and minimums before you decide whether to book.

## 51. Can I hire packers for only my kitchen?

Yes, partial packing can focus on a kitchen or another agreed room rather than the entire home. Tell Haul Bros about dishes, glassware, small appliances, pantry contents, and anything fragile. Confirm which materials and labor are included. Set aside food, valuables, and items you need before moving day. [Read our packing services guide.](professional-packing-services-northern-virginia/)

## 52. Are moving boxes and packing paper included with furniture protection?

Boxes and packing paper are different from the blankets or wrapping used to protect furniture during transport. Ask for a packing estimate that separates labor, ordinary cartons, and specialty materials. Do not assume a moving package includes packing every cabinet or supplying every box. Confirm the quantities, charges, and handling of unused supplies. [Read our packing services guide.](professional-packing-services-northern-virginia/)

## 53. Should I empty dresser drawers and filing cabinets before movers arrive?

Ask your mover about the specific furniture. Heavy contents, fragile pieces, loose items, and paperwork can make a cabinet unsafe to carry or damage its structure. Empty filing cabinets unless the crew approves another approach. Remove valuables and anything that can spill or shift, and do not assume a full dresser can be moved intact.

## 54. How should I label boxes for faster unloading?

Write the destination room and a short contents description on the top and at least one side. Use the same room names at both homes, and mark fragile or first-open boxes clearly. For multiple delivery stops, add the destination address or a distinct label. Avoid writing sensitive information on the outside of boxes.

## 55. What should go in a first-night box?

Pack toiletries, towels, bedding, basic dishes, a change of clothes, toilet paper, simple tools, and anything needed immediately at the new home. Keep medications, identity documents, keys, chargers, valuables, and essential work items with you. If delivery could take several days, prepare luggage for that period rather than relying on one overnight box.

## 56. Can I use my own boxes for professional packing?

Tell Haul Bros which boxes you have before the packing appointment. Clean, strong boxes in suitable sizes may be useful, but damaged cartons or thin retail packaging may need replacement. Ask whether the crew can use your supplies and how that affects material charges. Specialty items may still require specific cartons or protection.

## 57. How should I prepare a refrigerator, washer, or dryer for moving?

Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and confirm your mover’s preparation requirements. Appliances may need to be emptied, cleaned, dried, drained, or secured with transport hardware. Arrange qualified help for gas, electrical, or plumbing connections where needed. Do not assume transportation includes disconnecting, installing, or testing appliances at the new home.

## 58. Can movers take down wall-mounted TVs, shelves, and curtain rods?

Confirm removal and installation services separately from transportation. Wall mounts, electrical connections, anchors, and building rules can require a handyman or another qualified provider. Tell Haul Bros which items are attached to the property and have them ready as agreed. Keep mounting hardware labeled with the item, and protect the wall after removal.

## 59. How should I prepare houseplants for a move?

Ask the mover whether plants are accepted on your route and under what conditions. Plants can be damaged by temperature changes, spills, or time in a closed truck. Keep pots stable and avoid excess water immediately before transport. For interstate travel, check destination restrictions with the relevant agricultural authority before bringing plants.

## 60. Will unpacking include organizing cabinets and installing electronics?

Unpacking and home organization are different services. An unpacking appointment may cover opening boxes, placing items on available surfaces, and removing agreed packing debris. Confirm whether cabinet placement, shelving, electronics setup, or detailed organization is included. Give the crew clear instructions and identify areas that should remain untouched. [Read our packing services guide.](professional-packing-services-northern-virginia/)

## 61. Do I need to reserve street parking for a moving truck in Arlington?

If the move needs public curb space in Arlington, check the county’s moving-van or container permit process before moving day. A building reservation does not necessarily reserve public street parking. Confirm who applies, where the truck may stand, and the current posting requirements directly with the county. [Check Arlington’s moving-van permit guidance.](https://www.arlingtonva.us/Government/Programs/Building/Permits/Transportation-Right-of-Way/Moving-VanContainer-Permit)

## 62. How do I reserve moving-truck parking in the City of Alexandria?

The City of Alexandria provides a temporary reserved parking process that includes moving and loading uses. Check the city’s current application, fees, lead times, and sign requirements for your address. Coordinate with your property manager as well. An Alexandria mailing address alone does not confirm that a property is inside city jurisdiction. [Check Alexandria’s reserved-parking guidance.](https://www.alexandriava.gov/permits/how-to-obtain-temporary-reserved-parking)

## 64. How can I prepare for a townhouse move in Ashburn or Brambleton?

## 65. Does an HOA need to approve my moving date in Gainesville or Haymarket?

Requirements depend on the community and the property. Ask your HOA or manager whether it limits moving hours, truck parking, gate access, or use of common areas. Get the instructions before scheduling, then share them with Haul Bros. Do not assume that an available moving crew means the community will permit the move at that time.

## 66. What if the moving truck cannot fit in my driveway in Great Falls or Clifton?

Send photos and describe the driveway width, slope, surface, overhead clearance, and turnaround space. The crew must determine a safe loading position. A long carry or a different vehicle arrangement may affect the scope and price, so discuss access before booking. Do not promise that a large truck can enter based only on a street address.

## 67. How can I plan around I-95 traffic for a move from Woodbridge to Stafford?

Share your building access windows and preferred timing, and ask Haul Bros to plan the departure around the route and current conditions. Avoid scheduling an inflexible key handoff immediately after the estimated arrival. Traffic, incidents, and weather can change travel time, so a fixed driving duration should not be assumed.

## 68. Do I need a separate elevator reservation at my new apartment?

Usually you must coordinate access with each building independently. Ask both managers about reservations, deposits, loading entrances, and allowed hours. Make sure the delivery window leaves enough time after loading and travel. Give Haul Bros the contact details and access instructions for both properties so the plan accounts for the full move.

## 69. What should I do if my building elevator breaks on moving day?

Contact the building manager and Haul Bros immediately. Confirm whether another elevator, a revised reservation, or rescheduling is possible. Stair access may change safety requirements, crew needs, time, and charges. The team should review the options before proceeding; carrying every item by stairs may not be practical or safe.

## 70. Can I move within the same apartment building without a truck?

An internal move may be suitable for labor-only service, depending on inventory and access. Provide both unit numbers, floors, elevator details, and furniture dimensions. Confirm whether a truck or other equipment is needed and what minimum service applies. Haul Bros can review moving items between units or rooms as a separate agreed job.

## 71. Can I hire Haul Bros only to load my rental truck or storage container?

Labor-only help can cover approved loading or unloading work without transportation by Haul Bros. Provide the truck or container size, inventory, access, and any heavy items. Confirm equipment, materials, and the loading scope in writing. You remain responsible for arrangements with the rental or container company and for confirming transport requirements.

## 72. Who decides what size rental truck I need for labor-only moving?

The rental company supplies capacity guidance, and your mover can review the inventory to help identify space concerns. A room count alone may underestimate a full garage, storage unit, or bulky furniture. Confirm vehicle dimensions, weight limits, and access with the provider. Loading help does not guarantee that every item will fit into an undersized vehicle.

## 78. Can a family member coordinate a downsizing move for a senior?

## 79. How do I label furniture and boxes going to different family members?

Use a clear destination label on every item and provide a written list of delivery addresses. Set aside belongings that are staying at the property. Ask Haul Bros to review the stop order, access, and additional handling before booking. Clear ownership and delivery instructions are especially useful when downsizing or clearing an estate.

## 80. Can movers work while cleaners, painters, or flooring crews are in the home?

Coordinate the schedule so work areas and walkways stay safe and accessible. Wet paint, unfinished floors, tools, and shared entrances can slow or prevent moving work. Tell Haul Bros which contractors will be present and arrange separate work windows where possible. Confirm when floors or finishes can safely bear furniture and equipment.

## 81. Is a move from Northern Virginia to Richmond an interstate move?

It is generally an intrastate move if the shipment stays entirely in Virginia and is not part of a broader interstate movement. A move between Virginia addresses can be interstate if the shipment travels through another state. Confirm the route and classification with the carrier rather than relying only on the distance. [See FMCSA’s interstate-move definition.](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/protect-your-move/are-you-moving/what-interstate-move)

## 82. Where can I check whether a mover is authorized for an interstate move?

Use FMCSA’s official mover-selection and registration resources to check the carrier’s current status and complaint information. Match the legal company name and USDOT number to the company performing the move. A number shown on an advertisement is not, by itself, proof of current household-goods operating authority. [Use FMCSA’s mover-selection resources.](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/select-mover)

## 83. How can I tell whether I am booking with a moving broker or a carrier?

Ask who will physically transport your shipment and which company will appear on the moving documents. A broker arranges transportation; the carrier performs the transport. Request the legal names and registration details before paying. Verify each company’s role and authority through FMCSA rather than assuming the website you contacted owns the truck. [Read FMCSA’s movers-versus-brokers guide.](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/movers-vs-brokers)

## 84. What is the difference between a binding and non-binding moving estimate?

A binding estimate generally fixes the price for the listed shipment and services, subject to permitted changes. A non-binding estimate is not a guaranteed final total. Ask how inventory changes, requested services, and delivery charges are handled before signing. FMCSA explains the rules for interstate estimates, including delivery-payment limits and exceptions. [Read FMCSA’s estimate rules.](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/how-to/subpartD)

## 89. What would Released Value protection pay for a lightweight expensive item?

For an interstate move under Released Value, carrier liability is limited to $0.60 per pound per article. A 10-pound item would therefore have a $6 liability limit, even if its purchase price was much higher. Review Full Value Protection and any separate insurance options before selecting coverage. [Review FMCSA’s liability and protection guidance.](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/protect-your-move/are-you-moving/liability-protection)

## 90. What should I check when my long-distance shipment is delivered?

Follow the carrier’s written claims procedure promptly if a problem appears, and review the applicable deadlines. Do not assume a verbal report replaces a written claim.
