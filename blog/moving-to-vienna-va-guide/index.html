Thinking about moving to Vienna, Virginia?

Located in Fairfax County approximately 15 miles west of Washington, DC, Vienna combines established residential neighborhoods, a walkable town center, parks, local businesses, and access to major Northern Virginia employment centers.

Whether you're relocating from Arlington, Alexandria, Woodbridge, Fairfax, Reston, Maryland, or another community, Vienna offers a distinctive lifestyle—but also some important financial and logistical considerations.

Housing costs are relatively high, property boundaries matter, and commuting conditions can vary significantly depending on your destination and schedule.

At Haul Bros Moving Co LLC, we help homeowners and renters relocate throughout Vienna and surrounding Northern Virginia communities.

This complete 2027 guide covers Vienna neighborhoods, home prices, rental costs, schools, transportation, lifestyle, and practical moving expenses.

Quick Answer: Is Vienna, VA a Good Place to Live in 2027?

Vienna can be an excellent choice for people who value an established suburban community, neighborhood parks, access to local shops and restaurants, and connections to the Washington metropolitan area.

However, buyers and renters should carefully evaluate housing affordability, commuting requirements, and the exact location of a prospective property.

1. Where Is Vienna, Virginia?

Vienna is an incorporated town within Fairfax County.

It is located near Tysons, Oakton, Merrifield, Dunn Loring, and Fairfax.

The town covers approximately 4.4 square miles, although the broader Vienna postal area extends beyond the incorporated town limits.

Major regional roads include:

  • Maple Avenue (Route 123)
  • Nutley Street
  • Chain Bridge Road
  • Interstate 66 nearby
  • The Capital Beltway (Interstate 495) nearby

Vienna's location provides access to employment centers in Tysons, Reston, Fairfax, Arlington, and Washington, DC.

Town of Vienna vs. Vienna Mailing Address

This distinction is especially important when buying a home.

Two properties with Vienna addresses may have different:

  • Municipal tax obligations.
  • Local service providers.
  • Zoning requirements.
  • School assignments.
  • Trash and recycling arrangements.
  • Neighborhood characteristics.

Use official town and county mapping tools to confirm which jurisdiction serves your property.

2. Best Neighborhoods and Areas to Consider in Vienna, VA

The best neighborhood depends on your housing budget, preferred property type, commute, and lifestyle.

The following are useful areas to research—not a ranking of school quality, safety, or investment returns.

Vienna Woods

Vienna Woods is an established residential neighborhood with a mix of older houses, renovated properties, and newer replacement homes.

Why consider Vienna Woods?

  • Established residential streets.
  • Access to central Vienna amenities.
  • Mix of architectural styles.
  • Detached-home options.

Moving considerations: Older properties may have narrow driveways, mature trees, or limited space for a large moving truck.

Westbriar

Westbriar is another established residential area associated with Vienna.

Homebuyers may find a variety of detached homes, depending on current listings.

Why consider Westbriar?

  • Established neighborhood setting.
  • Access to nearby roads and services.
  • Opportunities to compare existing and updated houses.

Moving considerations: Confirm driveway access and measure furniture before moving into homes with older floor plans.

Downtown Vienna and the Maple Avenue Area

The central Vienna area appeals to residents who value proximity to local businesses, restaurants, and community activities.

Housing options depend on the specific street and property.

Why consider central Vienna?

  • Local shopping and dining.
  • Community events.
  • Access to nearby parks.
  • Convenient access to the W&OD Trail.

Moving considerations: Commercial traffic, parking restrictions, and narrower access points may require advance planning.

North Vienna

Areas north of the town center provide additional detached-home neighborhoods and access toward Tysons and surrounding communities.

Why consider North Vienna?

  • Residential housing options.
  • Access to regional employment corridors.
  • Established streets and neighborhoods.

Moving considerations: Verify whether a particular property is within town limits.

Vienna Metro Area

The neighborhoods and residential developments around the Vienna/Fairfax–GMU Metro station are worth researching for people who prefer transit access.

The station is outside the incorporated town center, so it should not be confused with downtown Vienna.

Why consider the Metro area?

  • Orange Line transit access.
  • Condominiums and other housing options nearby.
  • Potential convenience for rail commuters.

Moving considerations: Apartments and condominiums may require elevator reservations, loading-area coordination, or certificates of insurance.

Wolf Trap and Surrounding Vienna-Address Communities

The broader Vienna mailing area includes residential communities around Wolf Trap and nearby Fairfax County neighborhoods.

These locations can offer a different housing environment from the incorporated town.

Moving considerations: Some properties have larger driveways or longer access routes, while others have steep entrances or multiple staircases.

How to Choose the Right Vienna Neighborhood

Before signing a lease or purchase agreement, compare the exact property address, monthly housing cost, school assignment, parking, transit access, and daily commute.

3. How Much Do Homes Cost in Vienna, VA?

Vienna is generally a high-cost housing market.

Housing reports available in October 2026 showed:

  • Approximately $1.36 million median home sale price in Redfin's Vienna market reporting for the three months ending September 2026.
  • Approximately $1.16 million typical home value in Zillow's Vienna home-value index, with data through August 31, 2026.

These figures measure different things. Median sale price reflects properties that sold during the reporting period, while Zillow's home-value index is a modeled estimate of typical property values.

They should not be treated as interchangeable or as forecasts for 2027.

4. How Much Does It Cost to Rent in Vienna?

Vienna also has a relatively expensive rental market.

Zillow's October 13, 2026 rental reporting indicated an average advertised rent of approximately $3,300 per month for its Vienna market.

That is a broad market indicator, not the guaranteed rent for a particular apartment or house.

Rental Housing Options

Apartments: Consider the monthly rent, parking charges, building amenities, pet fees, and lease terms.

Condominiums: Confirm landlord rules, building access, elevator reservations, and move-in procedures.

Townhouses: Review HOA requirements, parking, utilities, and maintenance responsibilities.

Detached Houses: Consider landscaping responsibilities, utility expenses, and the security deposit.

Questions to Ask Before Renting

  • What is the total monthly payment?
  • Which utilities are included?
  • Is parking assigned?
  • Are pets permitted?
  • Are there move-in fees?
  • Does the building require an elevator reservation?
  • What is the lease termination policy?
  • Are there restrictions on moving trucks?

5. Cost of Living in Vienna, VA

Housing is one of the most significant costs of living in Vienna.

Other expenses can include:

  • Property taxes or rent.
  • Utilities.
  • Transportation.
  • Vehicle ownership.
  • Childcare.
  • Groceries.
  • Insurance.
  • HOA or condominium fees.
  • Home maintenance.

Property Taxes in Vienna

Homeowners inside the incorporated Town of Vienna may be subject to both Fairfax County and Town of Vienna real estate taxes.

The town's official tax information listed a 2026 town real estate tax rate of $0.19 per $100 of assessed value. Tax rates can change, and the applicable county rate must be considered separately.

Properties with Vienna mailing addresses outside the town limits may have different municipal tax obligations.

Always verify the current assessment and tax rates before purchasing.

Is Vienna More Expensive Than Woodbridge?

Vienna generally has higher home values than many Woodbridge-area neighborhoods, although specific properties vary considerably.

A fair comparison should consider the same home type, square footage, condition, and location.

For households deciding between Vienna and Woodbridge, the financial trade-off may involve housing cost versus commuting convenience and neighborhood preferences.

6. Schools in Vienna, VA

Vienna-area public schools are part of Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS).

Schools associated with portions of the broader Vienna area include:

  • Vienna Elementary School.
  • Louise Archer Elementary School.
  • Marshall Road Elementary School.
  • Thoreau Middle School.
  • Kilmer Middle School.
  • Madison High School.
  • Marshall High School.

Important: This is not a school assignment list for every Vienna address.

School boundaries can vary by street, and a Vienna postal address does not guarantee attendance at any particular school.

How to Verify School Assignments

Use the official FCPS boundary locator and enter the exact residential address.

Families should also review enrollment procedures, transportation eligibility, and any specialized program requirements.

Private Schools and Early Childhood Education

The Vienna area also offers private educational and childcare options.

Compare program availability, tuition, hours, transportation, and admission requirements directly with individual providers.

7. Commuting From Vienna to Washington, DC, Tysons, and Fairfax

Vienna's location is one of its important practical advantages, but traffic conditions matter.

Driving

Major routes serving the broader area include Interstate 66, Interstate 495, Route 123, and local connector roads.

Commuting time depends on:

  • Departure time.
  • Destination.
  • Traffic.
  • Weather.
  • Road incidents.
  • Toll or express-lane choices.

Avoid relying on a single advertised commute time.

Metrorail

The Vienna/Fairfax–GMU station serves the Orange Line and offers an alternative to driving for some regional commuters.

Check current parking arrangements, schedules, fares, and station access through WMATA.

Biking and Walking

The Washington & Old Dominion (W&OD) Trail passes through Vienna and provides recreational and transportation connections.

Walking and biking convenience varies significantly by address.

Is Vienna a Good Location for Remote Workers?

Vienna can suit remote and hybrid workers who want residential space while retaining access to major employment centers.

Before moving, verify internet availability at the exact property and evaluate whether the home has adequate office space.
