Moving a 5-Bedroom House: Costs, Crew Size & Complete 2027 Planning Guide

By Haul Bros Moving Co LLC · January 29, 2026

Moving a 5-bedroom house in Northern Virginia? Learn about moving costs, crew size, trucks, packing, timelines, and planning with Haul Bros Moving Co LLC.

Updated October 2026 | 2027 Moving Planning Guide

Moving a five-bedroom house is a major undertaking. Unlike a small apartment or two-bedroom townhouse, a large household move often involves substantial furniture, dozens or even hundreds of boxes, multiple floors, garage storage, and items that require special handling.

Whether you're relocating a five-bedroom home in Woodbridge, moving from Stafford to Fairfax, or preparing for a large residential move in Alexandria, Arlington, Manassas, or Fredericksburg, proper planning is essential.

One of the first questions homeowners ask is: How much does it cost to move a five-bedroom house?

Other important questions include how many movers you'll need, whether a 26-foot truck is large enough, how long the move will take, and whether professional packing is worth the investment.

At Haul Bros Moving Co LLC, we provide residential moving services throughout Northern Virginia, including larger household relocations.

This guide explains the factors that affect five-bedroom moving costs, crew size, transportation capacity, packing requirements, and how to prepare for moving day.

Quick Answer: How Much Does It Cost to Move a 5-Bedroom House?

For a local five-bedroom move in Northern Virginia, a preliminary planning scenario might involve four movers working for approximately 8–12 hours, but some homes will require additional crew members, multiple trucks or trips, or more than one day.

Using Haul Bros Moving Co LLC's standard four-mover rate:

  • First two hours: $695.
  • Each additional hour: $249.
  • Eight hours: $2,189.
  • Ten hours: $2,687.
  • Twelve hours: $3,185.

These amounts represent one four-mover crew-and-truck package at the stated rates. They do not include additional trucks, extra crews, separately quoted packing, materials, specialty handling, or other applicable services.

The actual cost of moving a five-bedroom home depends on the inventory, access, distance, crew configuration, and total work required.

For a reliable estimate, request an in-home or virtual pre-move assessment.

1. What Makes Moving a 5-Bedroom House Different?

A five-bedroom home often contains much more than five bedrooms of furniture.

Many larger households also have:

  • Multiple living rooms.
  • Dining rooms.
  • Home offices.
  • Basements.
  • Garages.
  • Outdoor furniture.
  • Exercise equipment.
  • Storage areas.
  • Large appliances.
  • Children's furniture.
  • Specialty belongings.

A moving crew must account for the entire property—not just the bedrooms.

Common Challenges

Large Furniture Inventories

Multiple beds, dressers, sofas, dining sets, and cabinets require additional handling.

Multiple Floors

Staircases can increase loading and unloading time.

More Packing

Larger households often contain substantial numbers of small belongings.

Truck Capacity

One moving truck may not accommodate everything.

Specialty Items

Pianos, safes, exercise machines, antiques, and oversized furniture may require additional planning.

Longer Working Hours

A large-home move can take most of a day or extend across multiple days.

2. How Many Movers Do You Need for a 5-Bedroom House?

For many five-bedroom homes, four movers may be a reasonable starting point for a crew assessment.

However, the ideal number depends on the size and contents of the property.

Four Movers

Potentially suitable for:

  • Moderately furnished five-bedroom homes.
  • Well-packed households.
  • Properties with manageable access.
  • Moves with flexible scheduling.
  • Projects where truck capacity and time have been assessed.

Five Movers

May be appropriate when:

  • There is a larger furniture inventory.
  • The home has multiple floors.
  • Faster loading and unloading are priorities.
  • Furniture handling is more demanding.

Six or More Movers

May be considered for:

  • Very large homes.
  • Extensive furniture inventories.
  • Multiple trucks.
  • Tight move-out deadlines.
  • Large amounts of heavy furniture.
  • Coordinated multiday relocations.

Is a Bigger Crew Always Better?

Not necessarily.

Additional movers may reduce labor time, but they also increase staffing costs and require enough space for everyone to work efficiently.

A detailed inventory review is the best way to choose an appropriate crew.

3. How Long Does It Take to Move a 5-Bedroom House?

Moving duration varies significantly.

For preliminary planning, consider these illustrative scenarios:

Household ConditionsPossible Planning Duration
Lightly furnished, fully packed, easy access6–8 hours
Moderately furnished, local move8–12 hours
Heavily furnished, multiple floors10–14+ hours
Large inventory requiring multiple loadsOne or more days

These are planning scenarios, not guaranteed completion times or measured regional averages.

Factors That Increase Moving Time

  • Unpacked household items.
  • Large furniture.
  • Multiple staircases.
  • Long carrying distances.
  • Difficult truck access.
  • Furniture disassembly.
  • Additional stops.
  • Multiple truckloads.
  • Specialty equipment requirements.
  • Longer travel distances.

Can a Five-Bedroom House Be Moved in One Day?

Yes, some five-bedroom homes can be moved in one day.

However, larger inventories or complicated access may require a multiday plan.

A pre-move inspection can help determine whether a single-day schedule is realistic.

4. Is a 26-Foot Moving Truck Big Enough for a 5-Bedroom House?

A 26-foot box truck is commonly used for residential moving, but its suitability depends on the actual shipment.

A 26-foot truck should not automatically be assumed large enough for every five-bedroom house.

A lightly furnished five-bedroom home may have a much smaller inventory than a heavily furnished four-bedroom property.

Factors That Affect Truck Capacity

  • Number of beds and mattresses.
  • Sectional sofas.
  • Dining furniture.
  • Large cabinets.
  • Appliances.
  • Number and size of boxes.
  • Garage equipment.
  • Outdoor furniture.
  • Weight of belongings.
  • Safe loading and weight limits.

What If Everything Does Not Fit?

The moving company may need to arrange:

  • A second trip.
  • An additional truck.
  • A larger approved transportation configuration.
  • A multiday moving schedule.

The correct solution depends on the inventory, driving distance, truck availability, and loading conditions.

Why a Pre-Move Inspection Matters

A detailed inspection helps estimate the shipment's volume and weight, identify oversized items, and determine whether additional transportation capacity is needed.

6. What Affects the Cost of Moving a Five-Bedroom House?

Home Size and Inventory

A five-bedroom house with minimal furniture may cost less to move than a four-bedroom house with extensive belongings.

Number of Movers

A larger crew may complete the job more quickly, but staffing costs also matter.

Moving Distance

Local transportation requirements differ from longer-distance relocations.

Number of Truckloads

A second truck or trip can significantly affect the plan.

Stairs

Moving furniture between multiple floors can increase labor time.

Packing Services

Professional packing adds labor and potentially material costs.

Specialty Items

Pianos, safes, marble furniture, and other heavy or delicate belongings may require specialized equipment and staffing.

Storage

Temporary storage or additional transportation stops can affect total costs.

Access Restrictions

Narrow driveways, parking restrictions, elevators, and long carrying distances can increase working time.

8. Should You Hire Professional Packers for a 5-Bedroom House?

Professional packing can be especially useful for larger households.

A five-bedroom home may contain belongings spread across bedrooms, living spaces, closets, offices, kitchens, and storage areas.

Full-Service Packing

Professional assistance preparing most eligible household belongings.

Partial Packing

Packing selected rooms or categories.

Kitchen Packing

Preparing dishes, cookware, glassware, and eligible kitchen belongings.

Fragile-Item Packing

Protective packing for appropriate mirrors, artwork, decorations, and other delicate items.

Unpacking Services

Assistance opening boxes and placing belongings in designated areas, depending on the agreed scope.

Is Professional Packing Worth It?

It may be worthwhile if:

  • You have limited time.
  • You are moving a large household.
  • You have fragile belongings.
  • You need assistance preparing multiple rooms.
  • You want to reduce the amount of physical work required.

Packing labor and supplies should be separately identified in your written quote unless explicitly included.

9. Moving a 5-Bedroom House With a Basement and Garage

Basements and garages are frequently underestimated during moving estimates.

Common Basement Items

  • Sectional sofas.
  • Entertainment centers.
  • Exercise equipment.
  • Storage bins.
  • Bookshelves.
  • Extra refrigerators.
  • Workshop equipment.

Common Garage Items

  • Tool cabinets.
  • Lawn equipment.
  • Bicycles.
  • Storage racks.
  • Outdoor furniture.
  • Seasonal decorations.
  • Heavy equipment.

Why These Areas Matter

A garage or basement can contain enough belongings to require substantial additional truck space.

Customers should include these areas during a pre-move walkthrough.

Important Reminder

Certain chemicals, fuels, compressed gases, and hazardous materials may be restricted from transportation.

Ask the moving company about prohibited items before moving day.

10. How to Prepare Large Furniture for Moving

Five-bedroom homes often contain multiple oversized furniture pieces.

Beds

Some bed frames need disassembly before transportation.

Sectional Sofas

Certain sectionals can be separated into individual components.

Dining Tables

Some tables may require leg removal or other preparation.

Entertainment Centers

Large units should be evaluated for safe handling.

Refrigerators

Appliances may require preparation before moving.

Exercise Equipment

Treadmills and other fitness equipment can be heavy and difficult to maneuver.

Pianos and Specialty Items

These should be disclosed before booking so the company can determine appropriate staffing and equipment.

Do not assume all specialty-item handling is included in a standard moving package.

11. How to Save Money on a Five-Bedroom Move

Declutter Early

Donate, sell, or remove belongings you no longer need.

Pack Nonfragile Items Yourself

If your schedule allows, preparing boxes in advance can reduce packing labor.

Create an Accurate Inventory

Unexpected additions can change the crew and truck requirements.

Prepare Furniture for Inspection

Identify items that require disassembly or specialty handling.

Confirm Parking

Make sure the moving truck can access both properties.

Plan the Right Crew

Choosing the appropriate crew may be more efficient than simply choosing the smallest available team.

Consider a Multiday Plan

For a very large household, separating packing and moving into different days may be more manageable.

Request a Written Estimate

Review all included services, assumptions, and additional charges before booking.

12. Eight-Week Checklist for Moving a 5-Bedroom House

Eight Weeks Before Moving

  • Create a room-by-room inventory.
  • Research moving companies.
  • Request an in-home or virtual inspection.
  • Review your moving budget.
  • Begin decluttering.

Six Weeks Before Moving

  • Select a moving company.
  • Confirm the tentative crew and truck plan.
  • Begin organizing storage areas.
  • Arrange donations or junk removal.
  • Order packing supplies if packing yourself.

Four Weeks Before Moving

  • Begin packing nonessential items.
  • Confirm moving dates.
  • Arrange utility transfers.
  • Review furniture measurements.
  • Identify specialty items.

Two Weeks Before Moving

  • Confirm the inventory.
  • Review truck parking arrangements.
  • Label boxes.
  • Prepare appliances according to instructions.
  • Confirm the moving schedule.

One Week Before Moving

  • Finish most packing.
  • Prepare essential belongings.
  • Confirm access at both properties.
  • Review the written estimate.
  • Separate important documents and valuables.

Moving Day

  • Meet the crew.
  • Review the inventory.
  • Identify fragile items.
  • Confirm furniture placement.
  • Check every room, closet, basement, attic, and garage before leaving.

Underestimating the Inventory

A large household can contain more belongings than expected.

Assuming One Truck Is Enough

Truck capacity should be assessed, not guessed.

Booking Too Small a Crew

Waiting Too Long to Pack

Packing a five-bedroom home can require significant preparation.

Forgetting the Garage or Basement

These areas can add substantial volume.

Not Disclosing Specialty Items

Heavy or delicate belongings may require additional equipment.

Ignoring Property Access

Parking and carrying distances can affect the moving schedule.

Relying Only on a Verbal Quote

A written estimate helps establish the agreed scope and pricing assumptions.

How much does it cost to move a five-bedroom house?

Is professional packing worth it for a five-bedroom house?

Can movers help remove unwanted furniture?

How early should I book a five-bedroom move?

Starting six to eight weeks ahead is a useful planning target, particularly for large households or popular moving dates.
