Local Moving

We move apartments, townhomes, condos, and single-family homes across Woodbridge, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities. Our crew protects your furniture, loads the truck, transports your belongings, and unloads them into the rooms you choose. Standard moves include furniture blankets, shrink wrap, and standard furniture disassembly and reassembly.   • Standard furniture disassembly & reassembly

Assembly & Disassembly

Large furniture often needs to come apart to fit through doorways or travel safely. As part of a standard move, our crew disassembles and reassembles standard furniture such as bed frames, tables, and desks when needed. We keep hardware organized with the matching item and position reassembled pieces in your chosen room. Tell us about bunk beds, complex modular systems, or unusual furniture before moving day so we can confirm the tools, time, and scope required.

  • Standard bed-frame disassembly & reassembly
  • Tables and desks prepared for transport
  • Organized hardware kept with matching furniture
  • Reassembly and placement at your new address
  • Advance review of complex or specialty furniture
Included on All Standard Moves
