Moving from Woodbridge to Ashburn: What’s the Average Cost?

By Haul Bros Moving Co LLC · July 27, 2026

If you’re moving from Woodbridge to Ashburn, the price depends on more than the distance between the two homes. The amount of furniture and boxes, crew size, stairs, elevator access, parking, packing, and time needed to load and unload can all affect the total.

For a useful starting point, Haul Bros Moving Co LLC’s published local rate for two movers and a 26-foot truck is $395 for the first two hours, then $159 for each additional hour. That is the company’s rate—not a guaranteed total for every Woodbridge-to-Ashburn move. Your written estimate should reflect your inventory and access details.

Is Woodbridge to Ashburn a local move?

Both Woodbridge and Ashburn are in Northern Virginia, and Haul Bros Moving Co LLC lists both communities in its service area. The road distance depends on the exact pickup and delivery addresses and the route used. Traffic and the locations of the homes can affect travel time, so mileage alone doesn’t determine the move’s price.

What affects the cost?

The size of your move

A studio or small apartment may need less loading and unloading time than a multi-bedroom home. A detailed inventory helps the moving company estimate the crew and time needed.

Stairs, elevators, and walking distance

Tell your mover about flights of stairs, elevator reservations, long hallways, building rules, and the distance from the home to the truck. These details can affect the time required.

Parking and access

In Woodbridge or Ashburn, let the mover know about limited parking, loading areas, gates, or any restrictions on truck access. Share building instructions before moving day.

Packing and special items

Packing assistance, packing materials, extra stops, and oversized or unusually heavy items may affect the estimate. Ask which services and materials are included.

Crew size and total hours

A larger crew costs more per hour, but may suit a larger inventory or a tighter schedule. Compare the written estimate and expected work plan, not just the hourly rate.
