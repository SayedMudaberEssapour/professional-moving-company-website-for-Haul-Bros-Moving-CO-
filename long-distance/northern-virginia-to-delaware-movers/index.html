What Makes a Northern Virginia to Delaware Move Different?

Although Delaware is relatively close to Northern Virginia, crossing state lines makes this an interstate household-goods move. The correct transportation plan depends on where in Delaware you're going.

1. Different routes for northern and southern Delaware

DestinationGeneral route considerations
WilmingtonI-95 and appropriate local connections
NewarkI-95 and local roads serving Newark
MiddletownUS 301 and connecting highways may be relevant
DoverUS 301, Delaware Route 1, or other suitable connections
LewesRoutes toward coastal Sussex County
Rehoboth BeachDelaware Route 1 and local coastal access
Bethany BeachSouthern coastal highways and local roads

These are planning corridors, not turn-by-turn truck directions.

A route suitable for a passenger vehicle may not be appropriate for a commercial box truck. Verify truck height, weight, dimensions, restrictions, and the final approach before dispatch.

2. Delaware toll roads

Some routes to Delaware involve toll roads, including portions of I-95, US 301, and Delaware Route 1.

Tolls depend on the chosen route, vehicle configuration, payment method, and current rates. A multi-axle moving truck may pay a different amount from a passenger vehicle.

Your written estimate should specify whether tolls are included or separately charged.

3. Wilmington parking and apartment access

Moving into downtown Wilmington may require coordination with your building or the appropriate local parking authority.

Ask whether the property has a loading dock, reserved loading zone, freight elevator, or designated moving hours.

If the truck must occupy curb space or obstruct a lane, determine whether local authorization is required before moving day.

Do not assume that an ordinary parking space automatically permits commercial loading.

4. Newark student move-in schedules

If you're moving near the University of Delaware, confirm the exact date when you can access your new residence.

Student housing and apartment communities may use scheduled move-in periods, designated unloading areas, or specific building entrances.

Coordinate the delivery date with your housing provider before finalizing the moving agreement.

5. Dover and military relocations

Dover is home to Dover Air Force Base.

For military households, confirm the applicable PCS arrangements, installation access requirements, and whether the move is personally procured or arranged through the military.

A private moving estimate does not automatically establish eligibility for reimbursement.

Haul Bros offers a 5% military discount, subject to the company's applicable discount terms. The discount should be clearly reflected in the written estimate if applied.

6. Coastal Delaware moving conditions

Rehoboth Beach, Lewes, Bethany Beach, and nearby communities may experience heavy seasonal traffic and parking demand.

For summer moves, arrange the unloading location and property access early.

Some condominiums may restrict move-in days or hours. Historic or beachfront properties may also have narrow entrances, stairs, or limited space for a large truck.

7. Coastal storms and flooding

Delaware's coastal communities can experience heavy rain, tropical systems, coastal flooding, and other severe weather.

If you're moving during a significant weather event, road conditions and property access may change.

Review your moving agreement's scheduling provisions and keep important personal belongings with you.

8. Same-day moving considerations

Some Northern Virginia to Delaware moves may be feasible with pickup and delivery on the same day.

However, this depends on the shipment size, loading time, route, traffic, applicable driving requirements, and unloading conditions.

Do not assume same-day completion until it has been confirmed in writing.
