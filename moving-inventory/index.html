1. Tell us about your move

Calculate at your own pace. Contact details and a pickup location are required when you send your plan.

2. Add furniture and boxes

Quantities start at zero. Add boxes as well as furniture; use additional rooms for separate bedrooms or offices.

3. Your planning estimate

Planning ranges only, not a binding quote or booking. Haul Bros must review inventory, weight, access, route, and availability before confirming a written estimate. Specialty items and unusually large moves require review.

How the estimate works

Item volumes are approximate cubic feet. We add 15% loading allowance and assume 1,400 usable cubic feet per 26-foot truck; actual payload limits and item dimensions may require more trucks. Crew guidance uses inventory size. Time assumes 75 cubic feet per mover-hour for both loading and unloading combined, plus driving, setup, furniture assembly, access, and selected box packing. The range allows 20–30% variation. This is a planning model, not measured job performance.

For one-truck local moves: 2 movers $395 / first 2 hours + $159 per additional hour; 3 movers $495 + $189/hour; 4 movers $695 + $249/hour. Labor-only requests receive a custom written quote. A 2-hour minimum applies. Packing materials, specialty services, and multi-truck pricing need a written quote. Access changes estimated time, not a separate access fee.

4. Send your moving plan to Haul Bros

Send your contact details, inventory, access notes and planning estimate directly to our team. We’ll review the details and follow up about availability and a written estimate. Sending a request does not reserve a move.

By clicking “Send My Moving Plan,” you’re asking Haul Bros to contact you about this request. See our Privacy Policy.
