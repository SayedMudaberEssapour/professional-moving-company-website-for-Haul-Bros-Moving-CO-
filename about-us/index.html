Why Haul Bros Exists

Moving is never just boxes, furniture, and a truck. It can be a first apartment, growing family, new job, military relocation, college transition, fresh start, or decision to downsize. At Haul Bros Moving Co LLC, we help people through important moments with clear communication, honest estimates, careful handling, and genuine care.

Who We Are

We are a professional moving company serving Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C., Maryland, and surrounding communities. We help individuals, families, students, seniors, military members, and businesses with local residential, apartment, house, long-distance, commercial, packing, storage, specialty-item, and junk-removal services.
