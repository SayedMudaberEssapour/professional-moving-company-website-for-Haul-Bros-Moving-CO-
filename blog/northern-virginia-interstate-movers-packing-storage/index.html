Moving across state lines while arranging packing and storage can involve several schedules at once. Your current home may need to be cleared before the new one is ready, or you may need help packing the entire household before departure. This guide explains what to ask and how to assess whether a moving company is a good fit.

What to Confirm Before Choosing a Mover

When comparing moving companies in Northern Virginia, verify these details directly:

  • Interstate coverage: Does the company serve your exact pickup and delivery locations?
  • Operating authority: Is the company authorized for the interstate service it proposes?
  • Packing scope: Can it pack the whole home, selected rooms, or fragile items?
  • Storage arrangement: Is storage available, and who provides the facility?
  • Delivery plan: Can the company coordinate delivery when your new home is ready?
  • Written estimate: Does the estimate describe the services, pricing method, and terms?
  • Inventory and access: Has the company reviewed your belongings, stairs, elevators, parking, and special items?

Interstate moving requirements and estimates can differ from local moves. Request a written estimate for your exact route and inventory, and review the terms before signing.

How a Combined Move, Pack, and Storage Plan Can Work

A coordinated plan can be helpful when your move-out and move-in dates do not match. The steps may look like this:

  • 1. Share your route and dates. Provide both addresses, the preferred pickup date, and when you expect access to the new home.
  • 2. Review your inventory. List furniture, boxes, fragile items, and anything requiring special handling.
  • 3. Choose packing services. Specify whether you need full-home packing, selected rooms, or fragile-item packing.
  • 4. Confirm storage arrangements. Review the facility, expected storage period, fees, access, and delivery process.
  • 5. Review the written estimate. Check the scope, pricing method, dates or delivery window, and service terms.
  • 6. Confirm access details. Share stairs, elevators, parking, loading docks, and building requirements for each location.

The exact process, timing, and services depend on the route and the written agreement.

Questions to Ask About Full-Service Packing

“Full service” can mean different things from one company to another. Before booking, ask:

  • Does packing cover every room and the contents of closets, cabinets, and storage areas?
  • Are packing materials included or billed separately?
  • Are fragile items, artwork, electronics, and lamps included?
  • Is furniture disassembly and reassembly included?
  • Can the crew pack belongings before they go into storage?
  • Is unpacking available at the final destination?

Ask the mover to document the packing scope in your estimate so you understand what the crew will handle.

Questions to Ask About Storage

Storage options vary. Confirm:

  • Whether the facility is provided, arranged, or selected by you
  • Where the belongings will be stored
  • How storage is billed and what fees may apply
  • Whether you can access belongings during storage
  • How much notice is needed to schedule delivery
  • Whether the storage and transportation terms appear in your written paperwork

Do not assume that storage is automatically included in a moving quote. Review the specific agreement and facility terms.
