High-Rise Movers in Arlington

Certificate of Insurance for Arlington Moves

Some Arlington apartments, condos, offices, and managed buildings may request a Certificate of Insurance, commonly called a COI, from the moving company.

If your building requires one, obtain the building's insurance requirements and management information before moving day.

Haul Bros can provide a Certificate of Insurance when required and applicable.

Giving your mover advance notice is important because building management may need time to review the document.
