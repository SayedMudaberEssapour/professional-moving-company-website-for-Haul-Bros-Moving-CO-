Getting to Know Stafford, VA

Stafford is a county in Northern Virginia between Prince William County and the Fredericksburg area. “Stafford” may refer to Stafford County, the community around Stafford Courthouse, or a postal address elsewhere in the county. The county includes a range of residential settings, from suburban neighborhoods and apartment communities to rural properties and waterfront areas.

The county’s major north-south route is Interstate 95. U.S. Route 1 and state roads connect local communities, retail areas, schools, employment centers, and commuter options. Because Stafford covers a broad area, travel time between two addresses can vary considerably. Before you move, check the exact location, commute route, and access conditions for the home you are considering.

Stafford’s identity also reflects its natural and historical setting. Residents have access to the Potomac and Rappahannock river corridors, Aquia Creek, county parks, and historic sites such as Government Island and the Ferry Farm area. For daily life, the best fit depends on your particular address, work commute, household needs, and preferred pace of life.

Stafford Communities and Local Areas

Stafford County is made up of unincorporated communities and county neighborhoods rather than one incorporated city. Common area names include:

  • Stafford Courthouse: The county government and service area near Courthouse Road.
  • Garrisonville and North Stafford: Residential and commercial areas along the Garrisonville Road and Route 610 corridor, with access to I-95.
  • Aquia and Aquia Harbour: Communities near Aquia Creek, U.S. Route 1, and the eastern part of the county.
  • Falmouth: A historic community near the Rappahannock River and the Fredericksburg boundary.
  • Brooke and Widewater: Communities in eastern Stafford near the Potomac and Aquia Landing.
  • Hartwood and Rock Hill: More rural and suburban areas in western Stafford.
  • Griffis-Widewater: Residential areas extending toward Quantico and the Prince William County line.

Names used by residents, mailing addresses, school zones, and county planning districts do not necessarily share the same boundaries. Check the exact address with Stafford County and the relevant property manager, HOA, school division, and utility providers.

Getting Around Stafford

I-95, Route 1, and local roads

I-95 is the principal regional highway through Stafford. U.S. Route 1 and roads such as Garrisonville Road, Courthouse Road, Warrenton Road, and Brooke Road connect local communities to the interstate and to one another. Traffic, roadwork, incidents, school schedules, and weather may affect travel times, especially during busy commute periods.

For moving day, allow time for more than the drive between homes. The crew also needs time to load, navigate the neighborhood, park safely, use elevators or stairs, and unload. Tell Haul Bros about any gated entry, narrow driveway, low clearance, long carry, or parking restriction when you request an estimate.

Commuter rail

Virginia Railway Express (VRE) serves Stafford County at Brooke Station and Leeland Road Station on the Fredericksburg Line. Schedules, parking availability, and service alerts can change, so check current information directly with VRE when planning a commute.

Some homes are close to a station while others require a drive or connecting transportation. Confirm the specific route from your address rather than assuming a station is walkable.

Parks, Recreation, and Local Landmarks

Government Island

Government Island is a Stafford County park with trails and an elevated boardwalk through woods and wetlands. The site is associated with the local history of Aquia Creek sandstone, which was used in early government buildings in Washington, D.C. Check the county’s current visitor information before heading out.

Aquia Landing

Aquia Landing is a county park near Brooke Road with a beach area and picnic amenities. Access, hours, and seasonal conditions may vary, so check current Stafford County Parks information before visiting.

Widewater State Park and local waterways

Widewater State Park sits along the Potomac River in Stafford County and offers outdoor recreation. Aquia Creek, Potomac Creek, and the Rappahannock River also shape local landscapes and recreation opportunities. Review current state and county park details for trail, launch, reservation, and seasonal information.

Historic and community destinations

Stafford’s historic places include Government Island and Ferry Farm, George Washington’s boyhood home, near the Rappahannock River. The county also has community parks, sports facilities, libraries, and local shopping centers spread across its communities.

Moving to Stafford: A Practical Checklist

1. Confirm the address and jurisdiction

Verify the full street address, unit number, ZIP code, and county. Stafford mailing addresses can cover a large geographic area. Ask your leasing office, HOA, or property manager about rules that apply to moving trucks and move-in appointments.

2. Ask about community and building rules

Before scheduling movers, confirm:

  • Whether a moving truck can enter the neighborhood or community
  • Where a truck may park
  • Whether you need a reserved loading area or parking authorization
  • Elevator reservations and move-in time windows
  • Gate codes, call boxes, or security check-in
  • Low-clearance entrances or narrow roads
  • Any required certificate of insurance or building forms

Some rural properties have long private drives, gates, gravel access, or limited turning space. Describe these conditions clearly when asking for an estimate.

3. Share a complete inventory

List the furniture, appliances, boxes, garage items, outdoor furniture, and storage-unit contents you plan to move. Point out heavy or unusual pieces such as safes, pianos, treadmills, large sectionals, oversized mirrors, and commercial equipment. Photos can help a mover understand an item’s size and access path.

4. Plan for stairs and long carries

A home may have stairs at the entrance, between floors, or at a basement or garage. Apartment buildings may have elevators, but elevator distance and reservation windows still matter. Tell your mover about each flight, long hallway, outdoor path, and distance from truck parking to the entrance.

5. Think through the commute and timing

If you are commuting, test your route at the times you expect to travel and check current I-95 and local-road conditions. For moving day, coordinate key handoff, utility access, elevator windows, and arrival expectations at both homes.

6. Keep essentials accessible

Carry medications, valuables, important papers, keys, chargers, and a first-night bag with you. Keep these separate from the household goods being loaded onto the truck.

7. Get a written estimate and service details

A clear estimate should identify the crew, truck, pricing structure, how additional time is billed, services included, requested add-ons, deposit terms, and conditions that may change the total. Provide the same inventory and access details to every company you compare.

Frequently Asked Questions About Moving to Stafford, VA

Is Stafford, VA a good place to live?

Whether Stafford is a good fit depends on your commute, housing preferences, budget, and lifestyle. The county offers a mix of suburban and rural communities, commuter options, parks, and local services. Visit the specific area you are considering and verify current information about schools, transit, utilities, and housing.

Is Stafford a city or a county?

Stafford is a county in Virginia. “Stafford” is also used for communities and postal addresses within the county. Confirm which county or municipality serves your exact address when arranging utilities, schools, local services, and a move.

What are the main communities in Stafford County?

Commonly referenced communities include Stafford Courthouse, Garrisonville, North Stafford, Aquia, Aquia Harbour, Falmouth, Brooke, Widewater, Hartwood, and Rock Hill. Local boundaries and postal address areas may differ, so check the exact location.

How much do movers cost in Stafford, VA?

The cost depends on the crew size, total time, inventory, property access, and services requested. Haul Bros’ published local packages start at $395 for the first two hours with two movers and a 26-foot truck, followed by $159 per additional hour. Ask for a current written estimate based on your specific move.

Does Haul Bros Moving Co LLC move customers in Stafford?

Haul Bros serves Stafford and surrounding Northern Virginia communities, subject to route authorization, availability, and the written service agreement. Call 571-899-1919 or use haulbrosmoving.com to confirm your addresses and requested date.

How far in advance should I book movers in Stafford?

Contact a mover once your date is reasonably firm. Weekends, month-end dates, summer moves, and communities with limited move-in appointments may require more advance coordination. If your move is soon, ask Haul Bros about current availability.

What should I tell a moving company before moving day?

Provide both complete addresses, your date, inventory, stairs and elevators, parking or loading restrictions, gate information, long carries, and any heavy or fragile items. Tell the mover about private roads, gravel drives, low branches, or tight turns that could affect truck access.

Do Stafford apartment communities require elevator reservations?

Some managed apartment and condominium communities require an elevator reservation, loading-area appointment, insurance paperwork, or specific move-in hours. Ask your property manager for current instructions and share them with your moving company in advance.

Can I hire movers just to load or unload a rental truck?

Haul Bros offers labor-only assistance for qualifying moves. Explain whether you need loading, unloading, or both; the truck or container size; inventory; and access conditions so the company can confirm availability and provide an estimate.

Does Haul Bros provide packing services in Stafford?

Packing assistance may be available depending on your move and requested scope. Tell Haul Bros whether you need full packing, help with selected rooms, or assistance with fragile items. Confirm labor and material pricing in your written estimate.

Are there VRE stations in Stafford County?

Yes. VRE lists Brooke Station in Stafford and Leeland Road Station in Falmouth on the Fredericksburg Line. Check current station information, schedules, and service alerts directly with VRE.

What should I do the day before the movers arrive?

Finish packing what you plan to pack yourself, label boxes by room, set aside essentials and valuables, confirm parking and access, reserve elevators if needed, and send gate instructions to your mover. Keep walkways clear and make sure someone can provide access at both locations.
