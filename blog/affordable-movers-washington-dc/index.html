Many professionally managed buildings may request a Certificate of Insurance, commonly called a COI.

If your building requires one, ask management for:

  • Certificate holder information
  • Required coverage information
  • Additional insured requirements, if applicable
  • Building management contact
  • Submission deadline

Haul Bros can provide a Certificate of Insurance when required and applicable.

Send the requirements before moving day so there is enough time for coordination.

How Can You Save Money on a Washington, DC Move?

DC moving costs aren't only affected by how much furniture you own.

Access and preparation matter.

Reserve Your Elevator Early

Don't wait until moving day if your building requires a reservation.

Confirm Loading Access

Ask management exactly where the moving truck should load.

Handle Parking Requirements Early

Determine what parking arrangements or restrictions apply to your specific location.

Finish Packing Before the Movers Arrive

If you're packing yourself, have boxes sealed and ready.

Label Every Box

Label boxes by destination room.

Declutter

Don't pay to move belongings you no longer need.

Measure Large Furniture

This can be especially useful for rowhouses and properties with narrow stairs or entrances.

Provide Building Instructions

Send your mover elevator, loading dock, COI, and access requirements beforehand.

Provide an Accurate Inventory

Include all major furniture and specialty items.
