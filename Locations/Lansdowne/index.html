Local areas and community boundaries

Lansdowne is a Loudoun community often using Leesburg mailing addresses. The Lansdowne Boulevard and Riverside Parkway areas include different residential and business settings.

Nearby highways and local streets

Lansdowne Boulevard and Belmont Ridge Road connect the area with VA 7 to the south; Riverside Parkway serves local destinations.

Street access and moving-day planning

For a condominium or senior residence, confirm the loading entrance, elevator and approved moving hours with management. For a house, include driveway and basement details. A Leesburg mailing address does not automatically place the property inside the Town of Leesburg.

Population and density: 2020 Census

The 2020 Census counted 12,427 residents in Lansdowne census-designated place, with approximately 4.04 square miles of land. That is about 3,074 residents per square mile. These are 2020 figures for the Census boundary, not a current estimate or a count of everyone using the mailing address.

This density was higher than nearby Ashburn (3,030 residents per square mile in 2020). Population density provides local context; the actual driveway, curb space and building access determine the loading plan.
