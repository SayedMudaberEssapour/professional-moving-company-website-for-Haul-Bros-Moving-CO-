Ask your mover how much curb space the truck and loading equipment need. The city states that sign fees are not refunded unless signs were not posted.

Check whether your address is in the City of Alexandria

An Alexandria mailing address does not necessarily mean the property is inside city limits. Confirm the property’s jurisdiction before applying for city parking or choosing a local complaint office. Ask property management which rules apply at both addresses.

Old Town and older buildings

Tell your mover about narrow staircases, stoops, brick sidewalks, tight entrances, and any furniture that may need disassembly. These details affect handling, access, and crew planning. For apartments and condos in Carlyle, Potomac Yard, or along Duke Street, confirm elevator reservations, loading access, and moving hours with management.

How to vet an Alexandria mover

  • For interstate household moves, check registration and applicable operating authority using FMCSA’s mover search.
  • For moves within Virginia, check the appropriate Virginia DMV authority. Requirements generally distinguish moves of 30 miles or less from longer intrastate moves, with exemptions and authority-specific conditions.
  • Ask for evidence of applicable insurance and written shipment valuation and claims terms.
  • Get a written estimate showing minimum hours, crew, truck, travel, packing, and potential additional charges.
  • Compare recent reviews across platforms, including critical reviews and company responses. Check current ratings directly rather than relying on an old review count.

If something goes wrong: complaint assistance

Document the issue and contact the mover in writing. Keep estimates, contracts, receipts, messages, and damage photos. The Virginia Attorney General’s Consumer Protection Unit can help identify the appropriate consumer complaint channel. Call 1-800-552-9963 from Virginia or 804-786-2042 from Richmond or outside Virginia. Licensing checks and complaint resolution are separate processes.

Frequently asked questions

Is Alexandria a county?

No. Alexandria is an independent city with its own local parking and permit rules. Confirm that your property is within city limits; a mailing address alone is not enough.

How far ahead do I apply for reserved parking?

The city requires applications by noon, three full business days before the spaces are needed, excluding weekends and holidays. Check the city’s application timetable before applying.

How many spaces can I reserve for a move?

The city allows up to five spaces for moving, approximately 20 feet each. Confirm your truck and loading-space needs with your mover.

Where do I complain about a mover in Alexandria?

Contact the Virginia Attorney General’s Consumer Protection Unit for help identifying the appropriate complaint channel. Keep your contract, estimate, messages, and damage photos.
