How Can You Save Money on a Vienna Move?

Preparation can help reduce unnecessary moving time.

Declutter Before Moving

Donate, sell, recycle, or dispose of items you no longer need.

Finish Packing Before Moving Day

If you're packing yourself, have boxes sealed and ready before the crew arrives.

Label Everything

Label boxes with their destination rooms.

Reserve Elevators Early

If your building requires an elevator reservation, arrange it before moving day.

Clear Hallways

Give movers clear access to furniture and boxes.

Provide an Accurate Inventory

Tell your mover about all major furniture and specialty items beforehand.
