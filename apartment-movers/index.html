Apartment Moving Frequently Asked Questions

Do you move apartments on upper floors?

Yes. We assist with stairs, elevators, hallways, and longer walking distances.

Can you reserve the elevator?

Customers reserve elevators with building management and provide us the confirmed instructions.

Do buildings require a Certificate of Insurance?

Some do. Confirm your building’s requirements and provide the information before moving day.

Can you pack and store apartment belongings?

Yes. We offer packing, unpacking, storage transportation, and storage solutions.

Do you offer last-minute apartment moves?

Availability depends on the date, crew, and move size. Contact us as soon as possible.

How do I receive an estimate?
