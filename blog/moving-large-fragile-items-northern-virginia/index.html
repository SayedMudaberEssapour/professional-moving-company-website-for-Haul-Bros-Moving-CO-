Quick answer: To prepare large or fragile belongings for a move, make an item list, share dimensions and photos with your moving company, and confirm the packing and access plan in advance. Televisions, mirrors, glass furniture, and oversized items may need specific materials, extra crew members, or special arrangements.

Moving a large or delicate item takes more planning than carrying an ordinary box. A television may need a properly sized protective box, a mirror may need careful handling, and a glass table may need its pieces separated. Staircases, elevators, narrow hallways, and loading areas can also affect the plan.

This guide explains how to prepare common large and fragile items and what to discuss with movers serving Woodbridge, Fairfax, Alexandria, Arlington, Manassas, and nearby Northern Virginia communities.

Make an Inventory Before Requesting an Estimate

List items that may need extra care or planning. Include approximate dimensions, weight if known, and whether the item is fragile, unusually heavy, or difficult to access.

Your list might include:

  • Large televisions and computer monitors
  • Mirrors, framed artwork, and glass décor
  • Glass tables, shelving, and display cabinets
  • Lamps and delicate household items
  • Large appliances
  • Pianos, safes, and other heavy specialty items

Photos can help your moving company understand the item and its route out of the home. Photograph the item and any tight turns, stairs, narrow doors, elevators, or long walks to the truck. Share the photos when you request your estimate.

How to Prepare a Television or Monitor

If you have the original box and inserts, keep them available. Otherwise, ask your mover whether a suitable TV box or packing service is available.

Before the move:

  1. Disconnect cables and accessories.
  2. Label cables if you will reconnect the equipment soon.
  3. Keep the remote, stand hardware, and other small parts together in a labeled bag.
  4. Tell your mover the screen size and whether the TV is wall-mounted.
  5. Confirm who will remove the TV or wall bracket and whether that service is included.

Avoid pressing on the screen. Follow the manufacturer’s guidance for positioning and transport, and ask your mover how the television should be packed.

How to Prepare Mirrors and Framed Artwork

Tell your mover the approximate dimensions of large mirrors and framed artwork. Mention whether the item has a glass front, an unusual shape, a delicate frame, or special value.

If you are packing it yourself, use sturdy materials designed to protect the item and mark the package clearly. Ask your mover how it should be positioned during transport. For an oversized or unusually valuable piece, share photos and dimensions early so the company can confirm whether it can accommodate the item.

How to Prepare Glass Tables and Display Cabinets

If shelves, drawers, or table legs are designed to come off, remove them only if you can do so safely. Wrap and label separate pieces, and keep hardware in a marked bag. Tell your mover about fixed glass panels or components that cannot be removed.

Measure the furniture and the narrowest points on the route. Check doorways, hallways, stair turns, elevator openings, and the entrance at the destination. If you are unsure whether an item will fit, send the measurements and photos to your mover before moving day.

Share Access Details with Your Movers

The route from the room to the truck—and from the truck into the new home—can affect how a large item is moved. When requesting an estimate, mention:

  • Stairs, narrow hallways, tight corners, or low ceilings
  • Elevator size and reservation requirements
  • Long walks from the home to the truck
  • Loading dock, parking, or building restrictions
  • Items that are unusually heavy or awkward to carry
  • Packing, disassembly, or reassembly you may need

For apartment and condo moves in Northern Virginia, contact the building about elevator reservations, loading areas, and move-in hours. Confirm the requirements at both the pickup and delivery locations.

Questions to Ask Before Moving Day

Ask your moving company:

  • Can your crew move this item based on its size, weight, and condition?
  • Should I pack it, or can you provide packing materials or packing service?
  • Is removing it from a wall or disassembling it included?
  • Does it require additional crew members or special equipment?
  • Could the stairs, elevator, or route affect the plan?
  • Would photos or measurements help you prepare?

Company services and equipment vary. Confirm the plan in advance, especially for oversized, unusually heavy, or valuable items.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can movers transport a large mirror?

It depends on the mirror’s size, weight, condition, and access route. Provide approximate dimensions and photos when requesting an estimate so the moving company can review whether it can accommodate the item.

How should I prepare a television for moving?

Share the TV’s screen size and whether it is wall-mounted. Keep the original box if you have it, and ask your mover whether a TV box or packing service is available. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and avoid pressure on the screen.

Should I remove shelves from a glass cabinet?

If the shelves are removable and you can take them out safely, pack them separately and label them. Let your movers know about fixed glass panels or parts that cannot be removed.

What should I do if a large item may not fit through a doorway?

Measure the item and the tightest parts of the route, including doors, hallways, stair turns, and elevators. Send the measurements and photos to your moving company before moving day.

Should I tell movers about fragile or heavy items ahead of time?

Yes. Advance details help the company assess the item, access route, crew needs, and packing plan before the move.

Plan Ahead for a Smoother Move

Make an inventory, take photos, measure tight access points, and confirm the packing and handling plan before moving day. Clear information helps your moving company prepare and gives you time to address access issues or arrange another option if needed.
