Moving to Burke, Virginia? Whether you’re settling into a house, townhome, or apartment, a little advance planning can make moving day smoother. Confirm building or community rules, plan where the truck can park, and tell your mover about stairs, elevators, long carries, and fragile or oversized items.

Haul Bros Moving Co LLC is based in Woodbridge and serves Burke and surrounding Northern Virginia communities, subject to availability. Here’s a practical guide to planning a move to, from, or within Burke.

What to Know About Burke, Virginia

Burke is an unincorporated community in Fairfax County. The exact address matters when checking local services, schools, utilities, and community requirements, so verify those details with the relevant agency or property manager.

Burke Centre has a Virginia Railway Express (VRE) station. If you’re planning a commute, check current VRE schedules and service alerts directly with VRE, since schedules and service can change.

For moving day, share the full pickup and delivery addresses with your mover. Access and parking can vary by property, even within the same community.

Moving Checklist for Burke

Six to Eight Weeks Before Moving

  • Confirm your move date and the addresses for pickup and delivery.
  • Ask your HOA, leasing office, or property manager about moving rules.
  • Check whether you need to reserve a loading area, elevator, or parking space.
  • Request written estimates and compare the services included.
  • Sort items you plan to keep, donate, sell, store, or discard.
  • Decide whether you want packing help for all or part of your home.

Two to Four Weeks Before Moving

  • Confirm the crew size, services, and estimate in writing.
  • Tell your mover about fragile, heavy, oversized, or specialty items.
  • Arrange mail forwarding and update your address with relevant providers.
  • Contact utility providers for your specific addresses.
  • Reserve building access or parking if required.
  • Make a plan for pets, children, medications, and valuables on moving day.

Moving Week

  • Reconfirm the arrival window and access instructions with your moving company.
  • Keep walkways clear and set aside items that are not going on the truck.
  • Label boxes by room and mark fragile boxes clearly.
  • Keep identification, keys, chargers, medications, and important documents with you.
  • Take photos of the home’s condition before moving out, if useful.

Planning for Different Types of Homes

Moving from a House

Homes can have a larger inventory and items spread across multiple floors, garages, basements, or outdoor spaces. Share an inventory or photos with your house movers and mention any large appliances, patio furniture, storage areas, or difficult access points.

Moving from a Townhome

Tell your mover about the number of floors, stairs, parking location, and distance between the home and the truck. If the community has an HOA or shared parking rules, confirm those arrangements before move day. See our townhouse moving guide.

Moving from an Apartment

Ask management about elevator reservations, loading docks, move-in time restrictions, and required documents such as a certificate of insurance (COI). Share those requirements and deadlines with your moving company as early as possible. Learn about our apartment moving services.

Moving Day Access and Timing

Stairs, elevators, long walks, parking restrictions, and the amount of furniture can affect how long a move takes. Provide those details when requesting an estimate so the mover can prepare an appropriate plan.

Burke is served by local roads and regional routes, but traffic and road conditions can change. Allow flexibility in your schedule and check current conditions before travel. For VRE trips, consult VRE’s current schedules and alerts.

What should I tell movers before moving from a Burke townhome?

Share the number of floors, stair access, parking location, distance to the truck, inventory, and any HOA or community rules. These details help the moving company plan the crew and equipment.

Some apartment buildings and communities require advance reservations or have designated move times. Check with your property manager and pass the instructions to your mover.
