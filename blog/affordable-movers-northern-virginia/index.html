1. What does an affordable moving company actually mean?

The cheapest advertised hourly rate does not always result in the lowest final moving bill.

Imagine comparing two moving companies.

Company A advertises $99 per hour, but its written estimate includes separate charges for a truck, equipment, transportation, and other services.

Company B advertises a higher hourly rate but includes the truck, moving equipment, furniture protection, loading, transportation, and unloading in the quoted package.

These are hypothetical examples, but they illustrate why the total price matters more than the headline rate.

An affordable moving company should provide:

  • A clear written estimate.
  • Transparent hourly or flat-rate pricing.
  • An explanation of included services.
  • Appropriate operating credentials and insurance information.
  • A realistic estimate of the time required.
  • Clear policies regarding additional services and potential charges.

4. Seven ways to save money when hiring professional movers

Finding affordable movers isn't only about finding a lower hourly rate. Your preparation and scheduling can influence the total cost of your relocation.

1. Declutter before moving day

The fewer belongings you move, the less work your moving crew may need to complete.

Before your move, consider donating, selling, recycling, or responsibly disposing of unwanted items.

Focus on old furniture, unused appliances, broken household items, and belongings you no longer need.

Haul Bros also offers junk removal services that can be discussed separately when planning your relocation.

2. Pack your belongings before the movers arrive

Packing takes time.

If you're paying for hourly moving services, having boxes packed, sealed, and labeled before your crew arrives can help reduce the amount of work required on moving day.

Pack nonessential belongings several days in advance.

Keep important documents, medication, keys, and personal valuables with you.

If you need help, Haul Bros offers packing and unpacking services that can be included in a customized estimate.

3. Choose the right number of movers

Two movers may be appropriate for a small apartment with limited furniture.

Three movers may be more suitable for a two-bedroom apartment or townhouse.

Four movers may be useful for larger homes or moves involving heavier inventories and multiple floors.

The right crew depends on your specific move.

Ask your moving company to estimate the total job duration for each available crew size rather than selecting solely by hourly rate.

4. Schedule your move early

Contact moving companies as soon as you know your preferred moving date.

Early planning gives you more time to compare estimates, arrange building access, organize parking, and prepare your belongings.

You can also ask whether alternative dates or arrival windows offer different pricing.

Availability and any discounts should always be confirmed directly with the mover.

5. Reserve elevators and arrange parking

If you're moving into or out of an apartment building, contact your property manager before moving day.

Ask about:

  • Freight elevator reservations.
  • Loading dock availability.
  • Parking restrictions.
  • Certificate of insurance requirements.
  • Building moving hours.

Proper planning can help prevent delays that extend an hourly move.

5. Are cheap movers worth it? How to avoid hidden moving costs

Affordable movers can provide excellent value, but unusually low estimates deserve careful review.

Before hiring any moving company, ask for a complete written estimate that explains the services being provided.

Questions to ask before booking

1. Is the moving truck included?

2. Are furniture blankets and moving equipment included?

3. Are there separate charges for stairs or elevators?

4. Are fuel and transportation included?

5. When does billable time begin and end?

6. Is there a minimum number of hours?

7. How are additional hours billed?

8. Are packing supplies included or separately priced?

9. How are specialty items handled?

10. What happens if my inventory changes?

For interstate moves, the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration recommends obtaining written estimates, checking a mover's registration and complaint history, and understanding the services and liability options before signing a contract.

8. Is it cheaper to hire movers or rent a moving truck?

Renting a moving truck can be less expensive for some small moves, especially if you have help available and can safely handle the furniture yourself.

However, the truck rental price is only one part of the total cost.

You may also need to account for fuel, mileage, equipment rental, insurance or damage coverage, packing materials, and additional labor.

Professional movers may offer better overall value when your relocation involves heavy furniture, multiple flights of stairs, limited time, or complicated building access.
