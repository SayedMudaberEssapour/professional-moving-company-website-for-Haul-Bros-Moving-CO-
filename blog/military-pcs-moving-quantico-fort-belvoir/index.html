Military families often need to coordinate housing availability, shipment dates, temporary lodging, school enrollment, vehicle transportation, and the delivery of household goods.

Some families use government-arranged transportation. Others choose an authorized Personally Procured Move (PPM), commonly called a DITY move, or need additional privately arranged moving assistance.

Quick Answer: How Do You Plan a PCS Move Near Quantico or Fort Belvoir?

Start by reviewing your official PCS orders and contacting your installation's transportation or personal property office.

Before arranging a move, determine which transportation benefits and services you're authorized to use.

Seven Essential PCS Moving Steps

  1. Review your PCS orders and report date.
  2. Contact your installation's transportation or personal property office.
  3. Confirm whether you'll use government-arranged transportation, an approved PPM, or another authorized option.

1. What Is a Military PCS Move?

PCS stands for Permanent Change of Station.

A PCS move occurs when a service member receives orders assigning them to a different permanent duty station.

PCS relocations may involve moving:

  • Household furniture.
  • Clothing and personal belongings.
  • Kitchen items.
  • Children's furniture.
  • Home office equipment.
  • Approved appliances.
  • Other eligible household goods.

The services and allowances available depend on the orders, eligibility, destination, applicable regulations, and the approved transportation arrangement.

Common Types of PCS Relocations

CONUS PCS

A move within the continental United States.

OCONUS PCS

A move to or from a location outside the continental United States.

Government-Arranged Household Goods Move

An authorized shipment coordinated through the military personal property transportation program.

Personally Procured Move (PPM)

A move in which the service member arranges some or all of the authorized household goods transportation, subject to applicable approval and documentation requirements.

Partial PPM

A combination of government-arranged shipment and personally transported household goods, when authorized.

2. Understanding PPM and DITY Moves in 2027

A Personally Procured Move, or PPM, is commonly called a DITY move.

Under an authorized PPM, eligible service members arrange transportation of household goods themselves or through qualifying commercial services.

The military may provide an incentive payment or reimbursement according to the applicable rules and authorized shipment weight.

Why Do Some Military Families Choose a PPM?

Potential reasons include:

  • Greater control over scheduling.
  • Flexibility in packing.
  • Direct management of belongings.
  • The ability to use a rental truck.
  • The option to hire loading or unloading labor.
  • More involvement in the moving process.

Important PPM Requirements

Review the official PPM fact sheet and confirm the following with your transportation office before making commitments:

  • Eligibility under your orders.
  • Required authorization.
  • Authorized weight allowance.
  • Applicable incentive calculation.
  • Weight-ticket requirements.
  • Documentation and receipt requirements.
  • Claim submission procedures.
  • Whether specific commercial services qualify.

Can You Hire Movers for a PPM?

Yes, some authorized PPM arrangements involve commercial moving services.

However, the transportation office must clarify which expenses are eligible and how they affect your payment or claim.

What About the PPM Incentive?

The incentive is based on applicable military rules and a government-constructed transportation cost, not simply a percentage of a private moving company's invoice.

Do not assume a particular payment amount or that every expense will be reimbursed.

Best practice: Obtain a personalized PPM estimate and documentation instructions from your transportation office before spending money.

Woodbridge, VA

Neighborhoods worth researching include Dale City, Lake Ridge, and other residential areas.

Stafford, VA

Housing options vary across North Stafford, the Courthouse area, and surrounding communities.

Fredericksburg, VA

Fredericksburg is farther south and may appeal to households with different housing preferences or budgets.

However, commuting conditions should be evaluated carefully.

8. What Documents Do You Need for a Military PCS Move?

PCS documentation requirements depend on the branch of service, orders, and type of move.

Common documents to organize include:

  • PCS orders and amendments.
  • Government-issued identification.
  • Transportation office instructions.
  • Household goods shipment documents.
  • Approved PPM paperwork, if applicable.
  • Weight tickets when required.
  • Moving contracts.
  • Itemized invoices.
  • Payment receipts.
  • Housing and lease documents.
  • Relevant travel documentation.

Why Are Weight Tickets Important?

For many PPMs, certified empty and loaded vehicle weights are used to establish the weight of the household goods transported.

The transportation office can explain the correct weighing procedure, ticket information, and any exceptions applicable to your move. See Military OneSource’s moving FAQs for weight-ticket guidance.

Important Reminder

Do not begin an intended reimbursable PCS shipment before obtaining the required orders and approvals.

Follow the current instructions issued for your specific move.

  • Garage.

11. Moving Into Military Housing or an Apartment

Military families may move into on-installation housing, privately managed military housing, apartments, or civilian residential communities.

Each property can have different moving requirements.

Questions to Ask

  • What is the confirmed move-in date?
  • Are movers allowed during specific hours?
  • Is a gate pass or visitor authorization required?
  • Can the moving truck access the property?
  • Is an elevator reservation needed?
  • Are there parking restrictions?
  • Does the property require a certificate of insurance?
  • Are there move-in inspection procedures?

Installation Access

Access to military installations is controlled.

Civilian movers should not assume they can enter an installation without advance coordination.

The customer should confirm applicable vehicle, driver, identification, escort, and visitor requirements with the installation.

Haul Bros can discuss logistics for qualifying moves, but access must be approved by the installation.
