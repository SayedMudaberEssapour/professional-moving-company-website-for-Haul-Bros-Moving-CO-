North Stafford, central Stafford, and Falmouth

  • Aquia Harbour, Garrisonville, and Widewater: Tell your mover about gate access, driveway conditions, and any community requirements. For Aquia Harbour, confirm association rules before reserving a date.
  • Embrey Mill and the Colonial Forge area: Ask your HOA or property manager about truck parking, moving hours, and loading access.
  • Falmouth and Leeland: Share your exact address, route, and property-access details so your estimate accounts for both loading and delivery.

Timing a move around I-95 and the VRE

I-95 and Route 1 congestion can affect transportation time. Ask whether travel is billed, when the clock starts and stops, and how delays are handled. If you commute by train, plan your own availability around the VRE Fredericksburg Line, including Brooke and Leeland Road stations. Avoid relying on a guaranteed arrival time without confirming the mover’s arrival window.

Military households

Share fixed reporting dates, closing dates, and any installation-access requirements early. Discuss schedule flexibility and ask about military discount eligibility through our military moving service. Same-day availability depends on the crew, truck, and details of the move; confirm any discount in writing.

Stafford County or the City of Fredericksburg?

Fredericksburg is an independent city, separate from Stafford County. Check the jurisdiction at each address before arranging street access. For a move on city streets, Fredericksburg’s temporary no-parking guidance requires signs to be posted 24 to 36 hours before the restriction begins. Review the full application and posting process with the city. For other streets, confirm requirements with the responsible road authority or your HOA rather than assuming the city process applies.

How to vet a Stafford County mover

  • For interstate household moves, check registration and applicable authority through FMCSA’s mover search. A USDOT number alone does not establish authority for every service.
  • For Virginia moves, verify Virginia DMV operating authority. Generally, moves of 30 miles or less require Property Carrier authority and longer intrastate moves require Household Goods Carrier authority, unless exempt. A household goods carrier transporting only household goods does not need separate property authority for shorter moves.
  • Ask for applicable insurance evidence and shipment valuation and claim terms.
  • Get an estimate with the crew, truck, minimum hours, transportation charges, and included services in writing.
  • Compare recent reviews on several platforms. Check original pages for current ratings and counts.

If something goes wrong

Contact the mover in writing and retain the estimate, agreement, inventory, receipts, messages, and damage photos. Ask how to submit a claim and which deadlines apply. The Virginia Attorney General’s Consumer Protection Unit can help identify the appropriate complaint channel. Call 1-800-552-9963 from Virginia or 804-786-2042 from Richmond or outside Virginia. Checking authority and resolving a consumer complaint are separate processes.
